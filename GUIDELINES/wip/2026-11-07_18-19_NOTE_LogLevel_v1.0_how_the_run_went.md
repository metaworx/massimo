1. **D3-A, the template.** `packaging/env.template` stays as it is,
   without the commented `MASSIMO_LOG_LEVEL` line.
   The Debian package's `postinst` starts the service only once
   `/etc/massimo/env` differs from `/usr/share/massimo/env.template`,
   and `massimo install` (`packagedSettings`) compares the same way:
   a new line in the template would make the untouched settings file of
   an unconfigured box differ after an upgrade,
   and the service would start, serving the LAN without a token.
   `packaging/README.md` documents the variable instead, and says why
   the template does not carry it.
