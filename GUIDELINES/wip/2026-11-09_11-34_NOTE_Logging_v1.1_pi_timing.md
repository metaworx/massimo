# NOTE Logging v1.1: pi timing

The check that Block 3's Verification of
[AP Logging v1.1](2026-11-09_00-23_AP_Logging_log_sources_and_serials.md)
leaves to the owner's go (D12-A), run on 2026-11-09 under the owner's `EXEC Pi A + B`:
the times of the insert and of a page before and after migration 0009,
on a copy of the store of each of the owner's two boxes.
`docs/store.md` (section 2, "Speed", and section 5, `events`) carries them.

## How it was measured

- **The boxes:**
  - **Pi 1:** a Raspberry Pi 1 Model B Rev 2 (armv6l, one core, 426 MB, SD card),
    Raspbian 13, `massimo` v0.12.0-alpha.1.
  - **Pi 5:** a Raspberry Pi 5 Model B (arm64, four cores, 8 GB, SD card),
    Debian 13, `massimo` v0.12.0-alpha.1.
- **The copies:** the owner ran, on each box,
  `sudo -u massimo massimo -env /dev/null -data /var/lib/massimo store backup /var/tmp/massimo-copy.db`
  and gave the copy to `mdr`.
  The Pi 1's copy held 977 events (debug 15, info 640, warn 322),
  the Pi 5's 133 (debug 9, info 123, warn 1).
- **The binaries:** a store test, `TestPiTiming`, compiled with `go test -c`
  for each box from `a70c24e` (`main` before #122, schema 8)
  and from `7a32e61` (`main` after #124, schema 9).
  It is not committed: it opens a copy of the copy on the SD card
  (`/var/tmp`, not the `tmpfs` of `/tmp`), migrating it where the binary is newer;
  takes the newest 1000 events of the Cube with the most of them as templates;
  times 30 batches of 100 through `writeEvents`, the writer's own batch
  (`flushAt` is 100), and 10 pages of 200 each:
  every level; warnings and above; errors and above.
  It then grows the copy to 90,000 events with the templates, cycled,
  and times the same again.
- **The service and its file:** untouched.
  The store files kept their times, both services stayed active,
  and the copies and binaries were deleted after the run.

## The times

The median, with the 90th percentile and the largest where they matter.

| What | Pi 1, 8 | Pi 1, 9 | Pi 5, 8 | Pi 5, 9 |
|------|--------:|--------:|--------:|--------:|
| Batch of 100, the copy | 115 ms | 133 ms | 2.2 ms | 2.7 ms |
| Batch of 100, 90,000 events | 124 ms (p90 310, max 4.6 s) | 147 ms (p90 277, max 3.4 s) | 2.7 ms (max 114) | 3.4 ms (max 105) |
| Page of 200, 90,000 events | 59 ms | 80 ms | 1.0 ms | 1.5 ms |
| Page of 200 warnings, 90,000 events | - | 110 ms | - | 154 ms, reads every row |
| Page of 200 errors, 90,000 events | - | 11.4 s (5.5 to 16.0 s), reads every row | - | 153 ms, reads every row |
| Grown from the copy to 90,000 | 2 min 43 s | 3 min 2 s | 3.6 s | 4.4 s |
| Open, migration 0009 included at 9 | 1.0 s | 1.8 s | 26 ms | 23 ms |

- **The insert:** 0009's two indexes cost about a fifth more on both boxes,
  0.15 s for the writer's batch on the Pi 1:
  for 3000 events a day, about 4.5 s of the writer a day.
- **The largest batch** waits seconds on the Pi 1, at 8 as at 9:
  a checkpoint of the WAL on the SD card, not 0009.
- **The walk that reads every row** - a page of a level that has fewer than a
  page of rows, warnings on the Pi 5's data, errors on the Pi 1's - takes
  11 s on the Pi 1 at 90,000 events, and holds the store's one connection
  meanwhile, so every other request that needs the store waits.
  At the copy's 4000 events it took 149 ms:
  22 times the rows took 77 times as long.
  The plan's Analysis knew the walk ("a page reads past the rows below it"),
  but only its 140 ms on a PC; the remedy is for the owner to decide.

## Where the run went differently from the plan

1. **Two boxes, and arm64 beside armv6.**
   The plan named the Raspberry Pi 1 and GOARM=6;
   192.168.2.5 turned out to be a Pi 5.
   The owner chose both, so each box got its own build.
2. **The copy's command** needed `-env /dev/null`:
   v0.12.0-alpha.1 reads `.env` from the current directory,
   and the owner's home directory is closed to `massimo`, so the first attempt
   failed with "open .env: permission denied" on both boxes.
   A separate session takes that on.
3. **Beyond the plan's "a batch on 0008 and on 0009, and a page of warnings":**
   the copy grown to 90,000 events, since the copies are small;
   a page of every level, at 8 and 9;
   and a page of errors, where the copy's warnings are too many for the worst case.
4. **A first run on the Pi 5 was discarded:**
   it took its templates from the Cube with the lowest id, which had one event,
   a warning, so every inserted row was that warning.
   The run repeated with the Cube that has the most events.
5. **A dry run** of both binaries on the Pi 5, on a stand-in store of 600
   events made by the test, came before the owner's copy.
6. **The Pi 1's host key** was new to the development machine and was accepted
   on first use.
