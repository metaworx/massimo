# NOTE LostCube v1.1: keepalive answered

A measured fact for Block 3, from the reading the plan's Analysis asks for
("The keepalive's risk"), made on 2026-11-09 at the owner's `EXEC`.

## The reading

On the Pi 5 that holds the test Cube, as the owner's user, without sudo,
sending nothing to the Cube:
`ss -tion "dport = :62910"` every 5 s for 75 s, 18:40:21 to 18:41:36,
on the connection of the packaged `serve` to the test Cube.
The Cube's firmware was not read.

| What `ss` showed | Means |
|------------------|-------|
| `timer:(keepalive,…,0)` back to 15 s every 15 s | a probe every 15 s, Go's default, none left unanswered |
| `segs_out` and `segs_in` +1 each at every probe, `data_segs_out` unchanged | each probe answered, carrying no data |
| `lastack` back to about 0 every 15 s while `lastrcv` grew to 58 s | the answers are acknowledgements, not data |
| `retrans:0/1` throughout | nothing retransmitted |
| once a minute 4 bytes out, about 100 in | the poll, `l:` and its L line |

## What follows

The test Cube acknowledges keepalive probes,
so Block 3's setting (15 s idle, 5 s between probes, 3 probes)
does not drop a healthy connection between polls.
Block 3 can run as planned.
