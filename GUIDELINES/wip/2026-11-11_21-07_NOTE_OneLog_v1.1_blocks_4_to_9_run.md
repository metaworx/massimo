# NOTE OneLog v1.1: blocks 4 to 9 run

How Blocks 4 to 9 of AP OneLog went where they differ from the plan,
on the branch of its pull request, after the decisions of
`wip/2026-11-11_17-21_NOTE_OneLog_v1.1_events_view_and_mac_logs.md`.

1. **Block 4's test, under `-short`.**
   `TestEventsPageQuery` gave every 3001st row a command;
   the armv6 job runs it with 3000 rows, so its page by command found none.
   `test(store): A page by command finds its rows under -short too`
   gives every 301st one, the push after Block 4 alone.
2. **Migration 0014 indexes `audit.command_id`** (Block 9; 0014 is in no
   release yet): the command's page reads its audit row by it,
   and the prune of a command sets its audit rows' link to NULL,
   which read the whole audit for every command without the index.
3. **Block 5 keeps the Cube client's own lines** of a connection, a loss,
   a device paired and one forgotten: its tests describe how the client
   reports a loss by them, and the commands without a server use them.
   `cubelan.Options.EventLines`, which serve's client sets,
   puts them at debug, so that the event's line says each once.
   The event lines keep the messages of the lines they replace
   (`device state`, `cube connected`, `device paired`, `backup taken`,
   `update requested`, …), so that what greps the log finds them:
   `tools/lan-test` and `tools/ui-test` grep them.
   `cube found on the LAN` drops its `, not configured`,
   which `kind=cube_found` says now.
   A restart, a reboot or an update helper that could not start is an
   error line, as the line it replaces was.
   The warning of a restore's step the Cube discarded stays,
   for the budget it names.
4. **Block 6's new kinds have their texts at once**:
   `command`, `hold`, `login`, `login_failed`, `locked_out`,
   with the sources `queue` and `server`, in the API's source choice and
   the web UI's in all six languages, since `TestLogKinds` holds the
   server's and the page's kinds together; Block 9 kept the command's
   detail. Block 7 did the same for the proxy's eight kinds and the source
   `proxy`, Block 8 for `audit_pruned`.
5. **The audit's lines stay off the streams of a user without `audit`**,
   as off their pages (Block 6): a subscriber knows whether it may see
   them, and a resumed stream's replay leaves them out too.
6. **The queue tells of its holds** (`engine.Options.OnHold`) and of a
   discard it reset and sent again (`engine.Result.Retried`);
   the queue's own lines of an observed command and of a hold go to debug.
   The observer is one per Cube now, to write the Cube's lines.
7. **A proxy client's address is in its command's `request`** (Block 7),
   not in `origin` as the plan says:
   `origin` is the node id of the columns a sync will use.
   Its action is `proxy.radio`, `proxy.delete`, `proxy.metadata`, …;
   it has no audit row.
8. **A device's next state line names the command sent to it**
   within two minutes (Block 7), the API's as the proxy client's:
   the Discussion's "the device's new state" among a command's lines.
   The proxy reads the device of a client's `s:` line for it.
9. **`GET /api/v1/commands/{id}` takes the command's number** too
   (Block 9), as the log's `command` and the audit's `command_id` name it,
   and gives a caller with `audit` the command's audit row.
10. **CI.** The push of the Mac's log change met an outage of
    `security.ubuntu.com` in three jobs; their re-run an hour later passed.
    Before Block 5 was pushed, the UI suite found its pairing test grepping
    `device paired`, which item 3's messages keep.
