The owner's decision of 2026-11-12 (`EXEC D21-A + fix merge conflict`),
on the lines Blocks 5 to 7 put at debug where another part writes them.

**D21-A: a logger at debug, chosen once.**
The Cube client, the queue and the proxy chose each such line's level at
the call (`eventLevel`, `stepLevel`, a level variable),
which made the calls hard to read.
`internal/slogx.AtDebug` is a logger that writes every record at debug;
each part picks it once where another part logs the same
(`cubelan.Options.EventLines`, the queue's observer and `OnHold`,
the proxy's `OnEvent` and `Command`),
and the calls read as plain `Info` and `Warn` again,
with their messages, arguments and levels as before.
No line changes level: the queue's `proxy client's command sent` stays at
info, though the step's row of a proxy client's command now says it too.
Not taken: B, leaving the calls as they were;
C, dropping the client's lines, whose tests describe how it reports a loss.
