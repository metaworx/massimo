# NOTE Oidc v1.1: how blocks 6 to 9 ran

Where the run of Blocks 6 to 9 on `claude/oidc-links` went otherwise than the plan says,
under the owner's `PLAN++ Oidc` of 2026-11-13.

1. **A link's refusal has a fragment of its own**, `#oidc-link-error=<reason>`
   (`taken`, `group`, `failed`), beside `#oidc-linked=<key>`, which the plan named alone.
   A link comes back to a page that is signed in,
   whose sign-in card would never show an `#oidc-error=`;
   My profile shows it instead.
2. **The link's start is audited, the link itself logged.**
   `identity.link` is the start's action and writes no line under Configuration,
   as `login.start` writes none;
   the callback, which has no principal, logs `account linked` at Info,
   and a refusal at Warn with its reason.
3. **`secret.key` is written as `opaque.key` is**, `O_CREATE|O_EXCL` with mode 0600,
   removed again where the write fails,
   rather than by `internal/safefile`, which the plan named:
   the two key files of the store stay made alike.
4. **The groups were read in Block 7 already**, from the claim `groups`,
   for an identity's details; Block 8 made the claim the provider's choice.
5. **The sealing at the open skips a store migrated short of 0016**,
   which the store's migration tests open,
   and compares the secrets in Go rather than in the query:
   `TestNoFilterOnContent` forbids a filter on `client_secret`.
6. **One unexplained failure**:
   the first `go test ./...` of Block 6 failed once in `internal/server`,
   its test's name cut from the output;
   four runs after it, the race run among them, and every run since were green.
7. **Not checked against a real provider**:
   that Nextcloud's `groups` scope sends the group IDs rather than their display names,
   as `docs/oidc.md` says;
   the owner's sign-in through breno's Nextcloud is still to come.
