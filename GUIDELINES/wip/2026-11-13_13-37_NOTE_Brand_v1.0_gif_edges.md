# NOTE Brand v1.0: gif edges

The owner's decision of 2026-11-13, after examples of both ways
(`grayorange`'s turn at 80 ms, light and dark).

## The finding

A GIF frame maps every pixel to one of the drawing's own colours.
Where an orange hand meets the blue M, the smoothed edge pixels are
orange-blue mixes, and by colour distance most of them are nearest to the
gray backing: about 400 of 500 in one frame came out gray. Every hand had a
ragged gray outline - nearly black on dark pages - that changed from frame to
frame as the hand turned: a flicker.

## Decision D25-A

The GIF palette holds, beside the drawing's pure colours, seven in-between
shades for every pair of them, the same in every frame; edges inside the mark
are smoothed as in the WebP, and only the outer outline stays hard.
In the example, no gray pixel touched an orange one (before: 167 on light
pages, 56 on dark).

Rejected: D25-B, each pixel the colour covering most of it - clean colours,
but stair-stepped edges that crawl as the hand turns.

## With it

The palette's transparent index gets a colour of its own that no pixel can be
mapped to; today it repeats the first colour, which a tie could pick.
