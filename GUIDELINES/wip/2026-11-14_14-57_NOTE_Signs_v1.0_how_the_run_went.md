# NOTE Signs v1.0: how the run went

How the run of [AP Signs v1.0](2026-11-14_13-05_AP_Signs_one_sign_system.md)
went where it differed from the plan; its pull request is #246.

1. **The window's limits are the store's and the API's alone.**
   The settings file has no line for them: `stale_after`, which the plan
   named as the model, has none either, only a flag and the store.
   `window_warn_after` and `window_alert_after` apply at once.
2. **The limits reach the page as times, not as settings.**
   Every device view whose state says a window is open carries `window`
   (`open_since`, `warn_at`, `alert_at`): in the rooms, the devices,
   one device and the event stream.
   The page needs no setting a reader may not read, and tells the state
   by its clock, redrawing a tile when one of its windows passes a limit;
   `GET /api/v1/system` was not needed.
3. **Blinking is kept across redraws.**
   A redraw makes a new element of a sign, which would start "at first"
   again at every poll: each mark's animation starts back at when the
   sign with its key was first drawn (`keepBlink`), and a sign not drawn
   for a minute counts as new.
4. **The server's sign** polls at a click while connected or gone;
   the reload button and its text `header.reload` are gone. One who may
   install updates asks whether one is out at the first poll and every
   half hour after.
5. **Found on the way:** a badge of the kind `warn stale-badge` is a
   warning (`badgeState`), and a summary shows the worst state of a kind,
   not the first.
6. **The schemes' values:** contrast light `warn` #8F3D0B, `warn-mark`
   #B34D0F; contrast dark `warn` and `warn-mark` #FFB870, `bad` and
   `fill-bad` #FF5A50, text on it black; graphite takes the default's.
7. **The tests that counted** the tokens (20), the editor's colour inputs
   (40) and contrast badges (16), My settings' device switches (7) and
   rows (8) follow; the open window's test sets the window's times, as the
   suite runs longer than the 5 minutes after the fake's window opened.
8. **AP HeaderPages v1.1** (#242) put the version and the refresh
   button into `.top-facts`. The branch took main in by a merge, and the
   server's sign went there, with the time in `.sign-updated`: CI's
   short release-like version had let the sign stay beside the logo while
   the time wrapped alone at 390 px (`test_server_connected_and_update`),
   which the long version of a build here never did; the test now tries
   both.
9. **Verification**: `make ui-test` whole (700 passed), `go test ./...`,
   `make race`, `make cross`, `gofmt`, the three `go vet` and
   `GUIDELINES/shared/tools/check`; the signs looked at, light and dark,
   1100 and 390 px.
