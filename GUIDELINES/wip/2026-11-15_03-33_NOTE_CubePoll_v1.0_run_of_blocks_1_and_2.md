# NOTE CubePoll v1.0: run of blocks 1 and 2

Where the run of `PLAN++ D119-B` (2026-11-15) went differently from
AP CubePoll v1.0; pull request #276.

- **The lost Cube's time:** the Analysis says two unanswered polls mark
  a Cube lost after `2 × (poll + 10 s)`, 140 s → 40 s. That is the
  upper bound the hold reckons with (`kernelHold`). The loss itself is
  marked within two polls and ten seconds, as README.md says:
  130 s → 30 s. The README, the commit and the `Changed` fragment say
  30 s; the TL;DR gate's bullet had "about 40 s".
- **The `Changed` fragment** also names `fixed_by`, which the TL;DR
  gate's bullet left out.
- **A second default:** `server.New` set a poll interval of 0 to one
  minute of its own; it now takes `DefaultPollInterval`.
- **A unit that ends in a full stop:** German's short units ("10 Sek.")
  doubled the line's final stop; the line keeps one.
- **`main` merged** into the branch before the pull request (the
  register of HeaderAnchor, one plan file).
