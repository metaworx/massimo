# NOTE UiParts v1.0: how Block 3 went

Where Block 3, Guard, went differently from the plan, a decision taken
at its gate, and what verified it.

## What it made

`tools/ui-test/test_parts_guard.py`: a table of 27 regions of the page,
each with how to show it and open what it has; eight data sources, each
applied with the data as it was; and the checks that nothing the user
holds is taken away. A list, `NOT_MOVED`, names the regions that still
fail it, each with what it loses.

## A decision at the gate

**D176-D, where the guard runs** (the owner, 2026-11-15): in two of the
suite's four cases, 1100 px in English and 390 px in German.
Each width and each language is covered once, and each of CI's two UI
jobs runs the guard once, on a worker of its own beside the other files.

## Different from the plan

1. **Who did it.**
   A sub-agent on Opus 5.5 wrote it in a worktree of its own, without a
   commit; the commits were made from recorded trees after the gate.
2. **Nineteen of the 27 regions are checked from the start.**
   The plan had every region on the list at first. These nineteen pass
   today, so they are off it: they are filled in place, or drawn again
   only by the user's own action, which the guard does not apply.
3. **Eight regions are on the list:**
   - five fail the guard today: the room tiles, a tile's configuration,
     the House tile, the Cubes list and Cube settings;
   - three cannot be shown with the fixture: the header's problems
     list, the devices without a room and the sign-in dialog.
4. **One check more than the plan's four.**
   Identity, open and typed run again once the focus has left the
   region: a redraw put off while the user was in it comes then.
5. **Eight data sources, not four:** the plan's poll, the loads of Cubes
   and rooms and the state event, and with them the refresh, the log's
   alerts, the ages and the log's poll.
6. **`UI_GUARD_CHECK`** checks a region although it is on the list. It
   is how a block sees its region pass before it takes the line off.

## For the blocks that move a region

- **Cube settings depends on time today.** Its problem pill appears
  only once the fixture's open window is five minutes old. Block 5
  takes the region off the list, and needs a problem that is there
  from the start by then.
- **The three regions the fixture cannot show** need a way to be shown
  before their block can take them off the list.
- **The guard was written before the scripts were cut.** It reads the
  page, not the scripts, and passed on the merged tree unchanged.

## What verified it

- **That it bites:** with the room tiles and the Cubes list taken off
  the list it failed in all four cases, the tiles with 39 failures a
  case, the closed Cube panels with 6.
- **Before the decision,** in all four cases: 80 passed, 32 skipped;
  beside `test_rooms.py` and `test_cubes.py`, 188 passed.
- **After the merge of main,** in its two cases: 40 passed, 72 skipped,
  of which 56 are the two cases it does not run in, in 382 s on two
  workers. No request other than a GET was sent in any run.
- The guidelines' twelve checks pass.
- **Not measured:** what the guard adds to a CI job. The pull request's
  run is the first to show it.
