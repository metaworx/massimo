# NOTE UiParts v1.0: how Block 6 went

Where Block 6, Header, went differently from the plan, the decisions
taken at its gate, and what verified it.

## What it made

- **`ui/src/header/`:** the Cube choice with each Cube's icons, every
  Cube's problem list, the chosen Cube's age, the server's sign, the
  certificate's line and the home notice, as parts.
- **Shared parts:** `Picker`, the menu that chooses one item, and
  `MenuItem`; properties on `Dropdown`, `Icon` and `Notice`;
  `joinParts` and `kindsLabel`.
- **`ui/src/model/`,** a new place: what two regions read of the API's
  data.
- **`tools/ui-test/test_header_updates.py`:** the open problem list and
  the open Cube choice follow a poll.
- **The guard's list** holds five regions, where it held six.

## What `ui/src/model/` is

- **It knows the API's data and no region.** It holds no state and no
  part with hooks of its own. It may put shared parts together into a
  small piece of that data, as a Cube's network sign.
- **What goes there:** the meaning of the API's data that two regions
  read. Today: the chosen Cube, a Cube's host and port, its sign, its
  problems as an entry of the problem list, and what a found Cube is.
- **What does not:** what one region alone reads; anything with state;
  anything that is not about the API's data, which is a shared part.
- **It imports** from itself, the shared parts, Preact and the API's
  types alone (`TestModelKnowsNoRegion`).
- **No region imports another** (`TestRegionsKnowNoOtherRegion`).

## Different from the plan

1. **Who did it.**
   A sub-agent on Opus 5.5 wrote it in the session's own worktree,
   without a commit, and reworked eight points after a second reader
   had checked it for reuse; the commits were made from recorded trees
   after the gate.
2. **Five commits, not one:** the API's required fields; the model;
   the shared menu parts; the header; the translations test.
3. **The facts and their fitting stay plain,** by the owner's word,
   where the plan moved them. With them stay the version and the
   refresh button. The refresh button's tooltip is still computed by
   the plain code, the age beside it by the part.
4. **The Cube choice had the same fault as the problem list.** Open, it
   was left as it was. It follows the data now and keeps the focus.
5. **"The found Cubes' hint"** of `home.js` is the page's toast on main
   now. That is the page's chrome (Block 14) and stays plain.
6. **The server's sign stays a button written by hand.** An icon
   button has no part yet, the owner's coming change takes this button
   away, and the rooms need one first.
7. **Markup:** two places without a box of their own, for a part among
   plain siblings (the class `part-slot`), and an id on the Cube
   choice's place. Before its first draw the server's sign is absent.
8. **Three tests read the part,** where they read plain globals that
   are gone: the age's in `test_header.py`, the search's end in
   `test_discover.py` and `test_cubes.py`.
9. **Two answers of the API got a name,** `Discovery` and `CertStatus`,
   so that the parts can be held to them (D177-A).

## For the blocks that move a region

- **A part among plain siblings** is mounted into a `part-slot`.
- **A test that reads a part after a plain call waits:** Preact draws
  in a microtask. A test that writes into a part's element breaks it.
- **The rooms' move takes `deviceProblems` and `deviceIcon`** of
  `tiles.js` into `ui/src/model/`: the model calls them today, and it
  may not import a region.
- **The rooms meet these first,** and add them as properties:
  - a `MenuItem` with classes and an id;
  - a `Dropdown` that tells when it opens;
  - a `Dropdown` whose summary is named by its text;
  - an icon button;
  - an item of the problem list with a "since".
- **A click on any button of a dropdown's list closes it.** That is
  the plain `dropdown()` of `components.js`. An item that leaves the
  list open needs a way around it there.
