# NOTE UiParts v1.0: how Block 7 went

Where Block 7, Rooms, went differently from the plan,
the decisions taken at its gate, and what verified it.

## What it made

- **`ui/src/rooms/`:**
  a room's tile with its device list,
  the devices without a room and the House tile, as parts.
- **`ui/src/model/device.ts`:**
  a device's icon, an open window's state,
  a device's problems and the device as an entry of the problem list.
- **Shared parts:**
  `IconButton`, `Select`, `DateTime`, `Stepper` and `ResultLine`;
  `actionRunner` with `set` and `fade`, and `fader`; properties on `Dropdown`,
  `MenuItem`, `Switch` and `TextField`.
- **`tools/ui-test/test_room_updates.py`:**
  the spike's twenty cases of the tile and two of the House panel.
- **The guard's list** holds two regions, `room-config` and `sign-in`,
  where it held five.

## Decisions at the gate

The owner, 2026-11-16:

- **D184-A.**
  The virtual thermostat's two settings, typed and not saved,
  go when the device list closes, as before the block.
  While the list is open they now stay through a device's report.
- **D185-A.**
  `tiles.js`, which draws no tile any more, keeps its name,
  and the room facts of `rooms.js` that only the parts call stay there,
  until Block 15: other sessions change both on main.
  The facts are `staleText`, `roomActual`, `roomActualDevice`, `apiUntil`,
  `confirmItem`, `confirmFieldsText`, `confirmShown` and `confirmFieldName`.
- **By D181-A,** the plain builders this block left without a caller stay,
  marked, until Block 15: `stepper`, `problemsEl`, `kindIcons`,
  `entryItems` and `problemEntry`.

## Decided at the gate, for a plan of its own

At D184 the owner proposed marks for unsaved settings, for every settings form.
It is not part of this plan.
What the owner decided is recorded here until that plan holds it:

- **What is typed survives its form's closing,** for every form,
  until it is saved, reset or the page is left.
- **D186-A.**
  The Save button gets an amber sign when the form is left with unsaved changes,
  and 60 s after the last change.
  The nearest pill then shows a disk and names the part with the unsaved
  settings.
- **D187-A.**
  The parts only, each region as it becomes one:
  Cube settings and the virtual thermostat first,
  a room's configuration with Block 8.
- **D188.**
  Settings without a pill near them, as System settings and My settings,
  get a pill in their section, and a sign in the header's menu as well.
- **D189-A.**
  Any failed save turns the sign red, also a value the server refused.
  A save that went through, or a Cancel, clears it.
- **After a save** the sign is the OK check.
- **Save is paired** with a Reset, back to the stored values, or a Cancel,
  back to the stored values and the settings closed where that applies, or both;
  the form decides.
- **The signs stand before the button's text.**

## Different from the plan

1. **Who did it.**
   A sub-agent on Opus 5.5 wrote it in the session's own worktree,
   without a commit, and reworked thirteen points after a second
   reader had checked it for reuse; the commits were made from
   recorded trees after the gate.
2. **Six commits, not one:**
   the API's required fields; the model; the shared parts; the tile;
   the devices without a room; the House tile.
3. **One directory for all three.**
   They share the device row, the rooms' load and one plain entry,
   `renderRooms`, and no region may import another.
   The second reader agreed.
4. **`room-config` stays on the guard's list.**
   Checked on purpose, it fails once the focus has left:
   the fill that was put off replaces every element.
   `configFocused` and `deferred` stay for the configuration alone,
   until Block 8.
5. **The devices without a room are checked by the guard,** where the plan left
   them out for want of one in the fixture: their view is given one by a stub.
6. **A Go test was corrected.**
   The import check of the parts took the text key `vwt.from` for an import.
7. **Tests select by place,** not by a class made for them: Cancel as
   `.form-cancel` inside its form, Hold as the input of `.more-hold`.
   `test_icon_choice.py` sets the choice as the page does.
8. **The message line of the devices without a room** is said by the part's
   action runner; `showUnlistedResult` is gone.
9. **What a user may notice:**
   - Keep in a room's delete question puts the focus back on ⋯;
   - Cancel and Keep under a device without a room put it back on their button;
   - the virtual thermostat's two settings show the "changed meanwhile" notice;
   - a tile's own actions no longer fill its configuration again.
10. **Markup:**
    a span around a ⋯ item's label and around the Hold switch's;
    the shared footer's spans in the holiday form,
    the House panel and the virtual thermostat's form;
    ids that nothing read are gone,
    in the House panel and on the steppers' buttons.
11. **D177-A:**
    required now are a room's `id`, `name`, `ready` and `devices`,
    a device's `type`, `room_id` and `reported`,
    and an open window's three times.
    A state's fields are not:
    a virtual thermostat's state may hold `window_open` alone.
12. **Main got the lint of the parts' hooks during the block.**
    The branch was cut before it.
    The builder was held to it by a pre-check that ran main's lint over a copy
    of the parts with main's changes merged in.
    At the merge the rooms' counter is read with `useLoad()`,
    and `shared/menu.tsx` conflicted at one place.

## For the blocks that move a region

- **A part looks up no id it draws itself with `$()`:**
  `TestUIPage` reads the bundle for those.
  It uses a ref.
- **A tile's configuration is an Island** filled by a counter of `store.ts`
  and by `ui.config`.
  Block 8 replaces the Island and drops `configFocused` and `deferred` without
  touching the tile.
- **`pairDeviceForm`,**
  Block 9's, sits in an Island keyed by the Cube's rooms.
- **A shared part carries no property that exists for one caller's
  test,** and none without a user.
- **Named as gaps in `docs/ui.md`,** for the block that meets each:
  - a `Select` beside another control in one row;
  - a `ResultLine` with something after its text;
  - the conversion of a `DateTime`'s value to the API's time;
  - a device row's head, which the pairing draws a twin of;
  - choosing rooms, drawn two ways;
  - a room write's outcome in words, built in three places.
- **A problem-list item with a "since"** was not needed and is not written.
- **A branch cut before a lint** can be held to it without a merge,
  as in point 12.

## Defects found outside the block

Not fixed:

- The virtual thermostat's address field sits indented under its label,
  and its box reaches a few pixels past the row's right edge.
  It was so before the move.
- A device row's Repair…, focused,
  is built again when the device's name changes.
  Before the move the whole tile was.

## What verified it

- **The new test on the page before the change:**
  17 of its 22 tests failed, in the builder's run; they pass after it.
- **That the guard bites:**
  with a tile built again at every event on purpose,
  it failed in both its cases, in the builder's run.
- **Reuse:**
  a second reader against the plain code of the later blocks;
  thirteen points reworked.
- **Main was merged twice,** since it moved again before the push.
  After each merge:
  `make ui`, gofmt, `go vet` for linux, windows and darwin,
  `MASSIMO_ENV_FILE=/dev/null go test ./...`, `make cross`,
  `make ui-check` with the hooks lint,
  the types' `-check` and the guidelines' twelve checks pass.
- **UI tests after the first merge:**
  seventeen test files in all four cases, the guard among them:
  508 passed and 60 skipped, none failed.
  Before the merge the same files passed in the builder's run,
  and twelve of them here.
- **UI tests after the second merge:**
  six test files in all four cases, the rooms', the guard
  and the three main had changed: 372 passed and 60 skipped, none failed.
- **Not run:**
  the whole UI suite, which is CI's; `make race`; `make lan-test`.
