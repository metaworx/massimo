# NOTE UnsavedMarks v1.0: decisions on leaving

The two decisions the plan left open before Block 3, as the owner took them.

The owner, 2026-11-16:

- **D8-A.**
  On closing, reloading or leaving the page while a form is unsaved,
  the browser's own prompt asks; a browser allows nothing else there.
  Where the user stays, the page then shows its dialog with the list of the
  unsaved forms, each of which opens its form when clicked.
  At sign-out, where the page itself drops the drafts,
  its own dialog with the list asks first.
- **D9-A.**
  Moving inside the page, to another of its pages or to another Cube,
  asks nothing: the drafts are kept,
  and the pill, the section and the menu's sign say where they are.

Block 3 no longer waits.
