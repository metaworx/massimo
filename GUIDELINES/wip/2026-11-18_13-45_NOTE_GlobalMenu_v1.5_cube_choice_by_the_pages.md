# NOTE GlobalMenu v1.5: the Cube choice by the pages, its cube coloured

A decision beside [AP GlobalMenu v1.5](2026-11-15_11-39_AP_GlobalMenu_menu_pages_backups_and_age.md),
taken by the owner on 2026-11-18 after Blocks 14 to 16 were merged
(#362), looking at the header in the browser: the cube button stood
farther from ↑ ⌂ ☰ than they stand from each other, the closed Cube
choice showed the network sign after the name, and the cube was grey.

- **D199-A:** the Cube choice is part of the page buttons' group, as a
  button and as a select alike: 4 px from ↑, or from ⌂ while ↑ is not
  shown, as the pages are from each other. The 16 px of the row stay
  between the logo, the pile and the Cube choice.
- **D200-A:** the closed Cube choice shows the cube and the chosen
  Cube's name, no network sign. Its list's rows keep the sign after each
  name (D190-A), their cubes grey.
- **D201-A:** in the header's Cube choice, the button and the closed
  select, the cube takes the link's colour, green, amber or red, as the
  network sign had it; colour alone, the link in words its tooltip and
  its name for a screen reader, the link's mark in the pill where it is
  not connected. This changes D191-A for the header's Cube choice alone:
  everywhere else a Cube's cube stays in the text's colour.

They are built on a branch of their own from `main`, the owner's word,
in a pull request citing Blocks 14 and 16.
