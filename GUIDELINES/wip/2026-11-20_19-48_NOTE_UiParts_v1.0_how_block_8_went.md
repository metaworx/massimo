# NOTE UiParts v1.0: how block 8 went

Where Block 8, RoomConfig, went differently from the plan,
the choices taken in the run, and what verified it.
It was built together with AP UnsavedMarks' Block 4,
whose NOTE says how that block went.

## What it made

- **`ui/src/rooms/`:**
  a room's configuration as parts of its tile,
  `Config.tsx` with its tabs, `ConfigDevices.tsx` with the Devices tab,
  and `configuration.ts`, what the configuration keeps per room of its Cube
  and what each tab sends, line for line as `config.js` sent it.
- **The week programme editor** stays the element it is,
  made once in an `Island`, its two resets in another.
- **Gone:** `configFocused`, `deferred`, the focus-out timer,
  the configuration's counter, the plain island in the tile,
  `rerenderRoom` and `MassimoParts.rooms.room`,
  and the partial `config.html` with its template of the tab bar.
- **Shared parts:**
  `HelpButton` and `HelpText`, `Tabs` and `Choices`;
  `NumberField` and `numberNorm`;
  `help` and `cls` on `Field`, `required` on `TextField`,
  `row` and `inputRef` on `Select`, `help` on `Switch`,
  `iconCls` on `IconButton`.
- **The House panel's rooms** are a `Choices` of checkboxes with All.
- **The guard's list** holds one region, `sign-in`, where it held two.

## Different from the plan

1. **Who did it.**
   A sub-agent on Opus 5.5 wrote it in the session's own worktree,
   without a commit, and reworked eleven points after a second reader
   had checked it;
   the commits were made from recorded trees.
2. **Three commits with UnsavedMarks' Block 4:**
   the shared parts; the configuration as parts; its forms and their marks.
3. **The actions moved into the part** (`configuration.ts`),
   as Block 5 moved the Cubes' into `send.ts`:
   only the parts call them.
4. **What stayed in `config.js`:**
   `NAME_BYTES`, `byteLength`, the name a device is offered
   (`defaultDeviceName`, `freeNameNumber`), `markNameBytes` and `roomDeviceNames`,
   which `pairing.js` and `repair.js` call,
   and `weekKit`, the plain builders the plain editor is made with.
5. **The Copy tab's lists** use the classes of the shared `Choices`,
   `.choices`, `.choices-head` and `.choices-list`, where they were `copy-*`.
6. **A write the Cube discarded,** or that a device did not take,
   leaves the stored values as they were (UnsavedMarks.D23);
   the plain code took the values sent as stored either way.
7. **Behaviour beside the fix:**
   - a device's send no longer resets what is typed for the room's other devices;
   - Keep on a removal puts the focus back on Remove,
     and Keep on a move on the room's select;
   - every field that shows stored data says when it changed meanwhile;
   - `docs/ui.md` puts the radiator settings on the Devices tab, where the code had them.
8. **Tests that reached plain globals** draw a room by `MassimoParts.rooms.data`,
   since `rerenderRoom` is gone, and close the configuration by its button.

## What the second reading found

Eleven points, all reworked:

1. **A defect:**
   after the configuration was opened again,
   the Copy tab copied the week programme as it was at the opening,
   not the days just sent, to other rooms.
   The programme sent is now the stored one.
2. A write the Cube discarded counted as stored, and the failure did not show.
3. The programme's form was not told of an edit in the editor.
4. The House panel drew its rooms by hand beside the shared `Choices`.
5. A move's Yes did not wait for another write of the configuration.
6. Keep on a move dropped the focus.
7. The Copy tab was a settings form, with marks; it is a command (UnsavedMarks.D21).
8. Two fixed waits in a test.
9. Code without a user, and a type the API already has.
10. Help's shown state was kept twice, by the plain code and by the parts.
11. An example in `docs/ui.md` did not read as the page does.

## Decisions

Taken in the run under the owner's `EXEC++`, each the owner's to change:

- **D190.**
  The House panel's rooms are drawn by the shared `Choices` in this block,
  not a later one: the configuration's move was where the two lists met.
- **D191.**
  A wall thermostat's `display_actual` is sent only where it differs from
  what was loaded; the plain code sent `false` after a switch turned on and off.

## For the blocks that move a region

- **`shared/` gained** help, tabs, a choice of several and a number field;
  `docs/ui.md`, "How the page is built", has their rows.
- **`Choices` lacks** what the capabilities of a role (`roles.js`, `capGrid`)
  and of an integration (`integrations.js`, `capsBox`) need:
  a group with a legend, an option fixed on, a badge or a title per option.
  The block that moves them adds those properties.
- **Help's shown state is one,** `helpOpen` of `app.js`:
  a part reads and writes it and draws again by a signal of its own,
  so a key is drawn by one side only, as every key is today.
  The plain half goes with its last user.
- **`config.js` keeps** what pairing and the repairs call,
  until their blocks (9 and the repairs') move them.
