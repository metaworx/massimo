# NOTE UiParts v1.0: how block 9 went

Where Block 9, Pairing, went differently from the plan,
the choices taken in the run, and what verified it.
The repairs moved with it (D192).

## What it made

- **`ui/src/cubes/`:**
  a Cube's pairing as a part of its panel,
  `flow.ts`, the flow live by the Cube's serial and what it sends,
  as `pairing.js` sent it, and `Pairing.tsx`, which draws it.
  The countdown changes its text in place;
  a joined device's form state is made where the device is listed.
- **`ui/src/shared/`:**
  - `put.tsx`, `PutForm`, the form that puts a device into a room,
    which the pairing and the devices without a room both draw;
  - `repair.tsx`, Repair… and the box of repairs, live by scope,
    which a tile, a device's row and a Cube's panel draw;
  - `name.ts`, how long a name may be and the name a device is offered,
    moved from `config.js`;
  - `path.ts`, `cubePath`, and `cls.ts`, `hid`, one each;
  - a field's `limit`, the part of `markRange`;
    a free text and number field by `value` and `set`;
    `inputRef` on both; `rowCls` on `Switch`; `role` on `ResultLine`.
- **Gone:**
  `pairing.js`, `repair.js`, the partial `pairing.html`,
  their declarations in `ui/src/plain/`,
  `pairingUI`, `redrawPairing`, `redrawRepair`,
  the islands of the pairing, the repairs and the put form,
  and `roomRepairScope` of `tiles.js`.
- **`MassimoParts.signedOut()`** forgets the boxes of repairs,
  every Cube's pairing with its countdown and the Cubes' rooms,
  beside the forms.
- **Tests:**
  `test_pairing_updates.py` and `test_repair_updates.py`, new;
  the guard's new views of the pairing and the repairs;
  `test_repair.py` reworded where it read the page's globals.

## Different from the plan

1. **Who did it.**
   A sub-agent on Opus 5.5 wrote it in the session's own worktree,
   without a commit,
   and reworked eleven points after a second reader had checked it;
   the commits were made from recorded trees.
2. **The repairs in it, D192:**
   the plan names `pairing.js` alone.
3. **Two commits,** each with its CHANGELOG fragment:
   the pairing with the shared parts it needs, then the repairs with theirs.
   The first hand-back had a third step, the shared parts alone,
   without a fragment; it was folded in.
4. **What moved and what stayed plain:**
   `pairingEvent` and `pairingJoined`, which `app.js` calls,
   moved into `cubes.js` and call the part.
   `config.js` stays with the week programme editor's kit alone:
   its only caller is `Config.tsx`, but moving it there would declare
   the plain element builders to the parts.
5. **Markup:**
   - the pairing's and the put form's Cancel are `form-cancel`,
     where they were `pair-cancel` and `pair-device-cancel`;
   - a closed box of repairs draws nothing,
     where a hidden `.repair-box` held its place;
   - Repair… lost its `data-scope`; the islands' wrappers are gone.
6. **The form's texts are its own:**
   `put.…` and `help.putCopy` where they were `pairing.…`;
   `put.name` and `put.room` beside the configuration's;
   `repair.nameRequired`; `name.long` for the name's limit,
   where `cfg.nameLong` was.
7. **Required in the OpenAPI file, D177-A:**
   `PairingState`'s `active`, `until` and `paired`,
   `NewDevice`'s `type`, `rf_address` and `serial`,
   and `RepairPlan`'s `plan`, `steps`, `radiograms`, `duty_cost_pct`,
   `slots`, `held_slots`, `resets`, `wakeups`, `metadata` and `notes`,
   each checked against the route's Go code.
8. **`test_repair.py`** reads none of the page's globals,
   since `repairCost`, `freeNameNumber`, `defaultDeviceName` and
   `roomDeviceNames` are gone:
   - a preview's cost is said as the page says it, by `t()`;
   - the lowest free number, whatever the case, is shown by the name a
     listing offers among room 1's devices renamed by the test's reads;
   - a name offered without a room's name is shown through the UI;
   - the stub is `RepairStub` of `helpers.py`;
   - what is sent is checked as before.
9. **The gap of a device's head drawn twice** stays in `docs/ui.md`,
   with what is left of it:
   both lines already share `typeName` and `DeviceIds`,
   and their spans differ in their classes and what follows.

## What the second reading found

Eleven points, all reworked.

1. **A defect:**
   a repair's question came back after its preview was read again,
   after a run in the same box or a name given,
   and its Yes then sent the new plan, which nobody had been asked about.
   A question now goes wherever its preview is read again.
2. **A defect:**
   the boxes of repairs, with a question and its plan behind Yes,
   the pairing's flows and the shown Cube's rooms survived a sign-out
   and reached the next user, also after an expired session.
   `MassimoParts.signedOut()` now forgets them.
3. After a run, a box closed meanwhile still read its previews.
4. The put form used the pairing region's texts (D193).
5. Copies: a Cube's API path in three places, `hid` in two,
   the gap of the device's head dropped, the Cubes' rooms read in three
   places without a line in the gaps.
6. Two exports without a user, `limitAria` and `freeNameNumber`.
7. A signal written while drawing, a joined device's form state.
8. The put form's room followed the first room after a reload,
   where `pairDeviceForm` pinned it at its first drawing.
9. The tests copied the suite's helpers, shadowed a fixture,
   and had dropped the case of a name without a room's name.
10. The guard's new views were not shown to bite.
11. A step without a CHANGELOG fragment.

## Decisions

Taken in the run under the owner's `EXEC++`, each the owner's to change:

- **D192.**
  The repairs (`repair.js`, written after the plan and named in no block)
  move with this block:
  they meet the same two regions as the pairing, the rooms and the Cubes,
  and Block 15 needs every region moved.
- **D193.**
  A part with state and requests of its own that two regions draw lives in `shared/`:
  it knows the API's data and no region,
  no import, no text, no id or class of one,
  since `model/` holds no state.
  The other way would be a place of its own, with a Go test to hold it.
- **D194.**
  The pairing reads a Cube's rooms by its own GET, the shown Cube too,
  until Block 15 has the rooms as a signal.

## For the blocks still to come

- **`shared/` gained,** with its next users:
  - `Limit`, a field's limit, for `markRange` of `notify.js`;
  - `cubePath`, for `base()` of `app.js` and `backup.js`'s own;
  - `Question`, already there, for the `confirmInPlace` of `users.js`,
    `roles.js` and `providers.js`.
- **Whatever state holds a user's data,** a question or a typed value
  is cleared in `MassimoParts.signedOut()`,
  which a sign-out and another user's sign-in after a 401 call alike.
- **No step without a CHANGELOG fragment:**
  shared parts go into the step that first uses them.
- **Three readers of a Cube's rooms:**
  the pairing's `cubeRooms` (`cubes/flow.ts`),
  the log's `logState.cubeRooms` and `fetchRooms` of `app.js`;
  `docs/ui.md` names them among the gaps for the log's block and Block 15.
- **The guard's new views cannot fail on the old page:**
  the plain pairing and repairs were drawn again by their own reads,
  events and renames, none of the guard's data sources.
  A comment beside each says so and what it guards from now on;
  `test_pairing_updates.py`, `test_repair_updates.py` and
  `test_repair.py` cover those paths.
