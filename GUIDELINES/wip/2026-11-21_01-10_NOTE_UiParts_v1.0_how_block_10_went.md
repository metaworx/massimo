# NOTE UiParts v1.0: how block 10 went

Where Block 10, Admin, went differently from the plan,
the choices taken in the run, and what verified it.
It was built with the first part of AP UnsavedMarks' Block 5,
the sections' pills and the menu's sign,
whose NOTE says how that part went.

## What it made

- **`ui/src/admin/`:**
  System settings → Users, Roles, Capabilities for every role
  and Sign-in providers as parts,
  a panel per user, role and provider built once and updated in place:
  `Users.tsx`, `Tokens.tsx`, `Identities.tsx`, `Roles.tsx` and `Providers.tsx`,
  their one store, `store.ts`, and what each list sends,
  `sendUsers.ts`, `sendRoles.ts` and `sendProviders.ts`,
  line for line as the plain scripts sent it.
- **`ui/src/integrations/`:**
  System settings → Integrations, each request and integration built once;
  the approval's watch stops while the list is not visible.
- **`ui/src/backups/`:**
  Backup and restore, a box per Cube and place,
  on Cube settings the chosen Cube's and in System settings a fold per Cube;
  what a Cube has is kept by its serial,
  so that both places show the same restore.
- **`ui/src/chrome/`:**
  a section's pill, ☰'s mark and a menu item's disk,
  in places of their own that the plain chrome leaves alone (UnsavedMarks' NOTE).
- **`ui/src/shared/` gained:**
  - `session.ts`, the session's number, and `actionRunner`'s `bound`;
  - `BusyButton`, a button that sends a request, and `useSetAtOnce`,
    a switch saved at once, in `question.tsx`;
  - `Adder`, a new item's form; `FileButton`, the choice of a file;
    `KvList`, a list of facts;
  - `ChoiceGroup`, the frame `Choices` is drawn in,
    with `Choices` under a legend,
    and an option locked, with a tooltip and a badge;
  - `TextField`'s password by `value` and `set`, with its `autocomplete`;
  - `localOf` and `isoOf` in `time.ts`; `unsavedTone` in `forms.ts`;
  - `FoldHead`'s `after`; `cubePath` for Backup and restore too.
- **`ui/src/model/cap.ts`:**
  `capLabel`, what a capability allows,
  which the roles and the integrations both read.
- **The cut:**
  `users.js` into `users.js` and `profile.js`,
  `providers.js` into `providers.js` and `linked.js`;
  My profile stays plain in the two new scripts until Block 13.
- **Gone:**
  `integrations.js`, the partials `roles-panel.html`, `providers-panel.html`
  and `backups-panel.html`;
  `users.js`, `roles.js`, `providers.js` and `backup.js` keep the plain names
  other scripts call, as calls of the parts.
- **`MassimoParts.signedOut()`** raises the session's number first,
  then forgets the lists, the integrations and every Cube's backups.
- **Tests:**
  `test_admin_updates.py`, new;
  the guard's new views of the users, the providers,
  the integrations and the backups;
  `test_users.py`, `test_oidc.py`, `test_log_alerts.py`, `test_integrations.py`,
  `test_backup.py` and `test_sign_in_resend.py` brought to the parts' markup.

## Different from the plan

1. **Who did it.**
   A sub-agent on Opus 5.5 wrote it in the session's own worktree,
   without a commit.
   Two independent readers on Opus read it,
   one the users, roles, capabilities, providers, the pills and the menu's sign,
   the other the integrations and the backups,
   and then checked the rework.
   Three rounds of rework followed, of 29, 14 and 3 points;
   the commits were made from recorded trees.
   The builder also resolved the merge of main.
2. **Three steps, not one per list:**
   users, roles with the capabilities, and providers are one step,
   since they share one store and one directory:
   they read each other's data, the roles and the enabled providers;
   it carries the sections' pills, the menu's sign and the cut.
   Then integrations; then backups.
   The trees of the first two were made from the last by taking the later lists out,
   each building alone.
3. **The cut is in the first step,**
   made by `tools/ui-split` with new groups in its map:
   a step of moves alone carries no CHANGELOG fragment,
   which the strict check refuses.
   `backup.js` was not cut: both its places became parts.
4. **No one list part for the five.**
   Common are `Fold` and `FoldHead`, `useForm` with `FormFooter`,
   `useAsk` with `Question`, `Adder`, and an `actionRunner` per list;
   the integrations' two lists and the backups' folds do not fit one list part.
5. **A region `chrome/`** for the sections' pills,
   ☰'s mark and the menu items' disks,
   in places of their own (`part-slot`) that the plain chrome leaves alone;
   `log.js` hands the log's count and tone to it.
6. **Markup:**
   - Cancel buttons are `form-cancel`;
   - an identity's Change is a form with marks,
     and closes after a change that went through;
   - the integration's name and its maximum have footers with Reset;
   - the capabilities' names are gone from the inputs.
7. **Taken beyond the plain page's behaviour,** each from the second reading:
   - no new plan while a restore runs, and the Restore buttons off meanwhile;
   - a plan closes when its stored backup is gone;
   - a restore whose end a stream resync lost is shown as unseen
     (`restore.unseen`) and gives the buttons back;
   - an answer in flight at a sign-out is dropped (`shared/session.ts`).
8. **Required in the OpenAPI file, D177-A:**
   the fields the server always sends of User, Token, Identity, LoginProvider,
   Role, Capability, the roles' answer, Provider, Integration, IntegrationUser,
   the found installations, StoredBackup, BackupList,
   RestorePlan and RestoreStep, each checked against the Go code;
   `may` and `secret_lost` added to the file.
9. **The merge of main carried a change of main's into the part:**
   #384 made a user's session row say "this device" and "its key",
   and Revoke on "this device" sign out, in `tokenRow` of `users.js`,
   which this block had cut.
   The change is in `profile.js` as main wrote it and in `admin/Tokens.tsx`:
   `applyCaps` of `login.js` hands the page's own token to the part
   (`MassimoParts.users.me`), which keeps it as a signal;
   `revokeOwn` of `sendUsers.ts` signs out;
   the part has a test of its own.
10. **A part of one point of the second reading not done:**
    no test shows that a typed password or secret is never a draft.
    While a form is drawn,
    nothing on the page tells a draft from a part's own state;
    the sign-out tests cover what a user could lose.

## What the second reading found

Three rounds, 46 points, reworked but for the part of one said above.
The six defects:

1. **An item of a section's pill** opened nothing while its card was folded.
2. **An identity's Change form with a draft** left a disk that opened nothing
   once the identity was removed or a sign-in made it unchangeable,
   and in the second round still after its provider was removed.
3. **The integrations' approval watch** did not start again after another page was shown,
   and it read while the card was folded, where the plain one did not.
4. **No test** of the integrations forgotten at a sign-out,
   nor of the integrations or the backups for another user after an expired session.
5. **An answer that arrived after a sign-out**
   wrote into the store just forgotten:
   a restore's plan with its Send, the integrations' list,
   the watch's next timer.
6. **A switch saved at once** was disabled while its request ran
   and dropped the keyboard's focus.

The 40 smaller points:
reuse (`BusyButton`, `FileButton`, `capLabel` to `model/`, `ChoiceGroup`,
the password fields through `TextField`, `KvList`'s filter to the caller),
tests (sleeps, checks that could not fail, the registration limit), and words.

## Decisions

Taken in the run under the owner's `EXEC++`, each the owner's to change:

- **D195.**
  Tokens and identities (`admin/Tokens.tsx`, `admin/Identities.tsx`)
  and the users' API path stay in the region until Block 13 makes My profile a part,
  which moves them to `shared/` (D193);
  the gap in `docs/ui.md` says what the move needs.
- **D196.**
  The guard's new views for the users, the providers,
  the integrations and the backups are forward guards:
  none of the guard's sources drew these lists again,
  so they pass on the page before the change;
  the lists' own reads are covered by `test_admin_updates.py`.
- **D197.**
  The hole of an answer in flight at a sign-out
  is closed in this block's three stores only;
  the regions moved before have it too (below) and are left for Block 14,
  where the session's end and `api()` are.

UnsavedMarks.D24 to UnsavedMarks.D26 were taken in the same run and are that plan's;
its NOTE has them.
The scroll test's fix goes with this block's pull request as a commit of its own (below).

## For the blocks still to come

- **After a sign-out:**
  whatever state holds a user's data, a question or a typed value
  is cleared in `MassimoParts.signedOut()`,
  and every store write, message and timer that follows an `await`
  takes the session's number first (`session`, `stillSession`, `bound`).
  Tests for both, a sign-out and another user after an expired session,
  and a held route across each.
- **A question never outlives what it asked about:**
  it goes when its preview or plan is read again,
  when its item is gone from a load, at a change of Cube and at a sign-out.
- **A form's draft is forgotten** when what it belongs to is gone
  from a load that went through, also while its panel is closed:
  no unsaved mark names a form that cannot be opened.
- **An item of a pill opens its form in sight:**
  the card unfolded (`showCard`), the panel open, the focus in it,
  from any page.
- **A switch saved at once** uses `useSetAtOnce`, one per switch,
  is never disabled, and shows the user's position while its request runs
  and the stored one after.
- **No request the plain page did not send:**
  a closed panel, a folded card and a page not shown read nothing;
  a watch stops where the plain one stopped and starts again where it did.
- **No copy of a shared part's markup** in a region:
  the skeleton becomes an exported part of its file, as `ChoiceGroup`.
- **Tests:**
  no assertion that cannot fail
  (a typed value is never in `page.content()` or `innerHTML`);
  no fixed wait to show that nothing more is sent:
  wait for what follows and count;
  a test does not end while the page still reads for what its cleanup deletes
  (`reads` of `helpers.py`).
- **The regions moved before have the late-answer hole,**
  a store written after an `await` without the session's number:
  - the pairing's flow and a Cube's rooms (`cubes/flow.ts`);
  - a Cube's devices and the Cube's actions' runner,
    `cubes/store.ts` and `cubes/send.ts`;
  - a room's configuration (`rooms/configuration.ts`:
    the load, the programme's read, the writes);
  - the runners of `rooms/Devices.tsx` and `rooms/Unlisted.tsx`,
    and `rooms/House.tsx`;
  - the repairs' preview and run (`shared/repair.tsx`),
    the put form (`shared/put.tsx`) and the header's search (`header/send.ts`).

  The plain page had the same window.
- **Block 13 moves** `Tokens.tsx`, `Identities.tsx`, `userPath`
  and their request functions, `revokeOwn` among them, to `shared/`;
  it also gives `FileButton` what `appearance.js` needs of it:
  an id, and a name for its input that follows the language.
- **My profile's tokens and identities are drawn twice,**
  plain (`profile.js`, `linked.js`) and as parts, until Block 13.
- **The server takes ten registration requests per address in ten minutes:**
  a test file with real integration requests is bound by it
  where the four cases share one server.

## Beside the block: the scroll test

AP LogCursor's NOTE,
`GUIDELINES/wip/2026-11-20_23-50_NOTE_LogCursor_v1.0_how_the_run_went.md`,
says of its pull request #383 that
`test_scroll.py::test_a_jump_down_stays_where_it_went[1100-en]` failed twice
"on the room configuration's week programme left by earlier tests".
A sub-agent looked: it is not the room configuration.

- **The cause:**
  the test read where the page ends once.
  The header's notice of a radiogram pending,
  after an earlier test's radio write (the fake Cube answers 49 of 50 slots free),
  goes two minutes after the last write,
  when the server reconnects for the budget and the greeting says all 50 free.
  The page is then 63 px shorter at 1100 px;
  the jumps past its end are cut to it and read as going back.
- **Reproduced** with a write 116 s before the test,
  with the CI's message, "500 → 488, back, 525 → 488, back, 550 → 488, back",
  and gone with the end read at each jump.
- **The fix** is a commit of its own on this block's branch, the test alone:
  "test(ui-test): The jump test reads the page's end at each jump".
  The product is right: the notice goes once the Cube says its slots free.
- **Open to the same,** at lower risk, and not changed:
  `test_system_layout.py`, with two reads of the page's height;
  `test_scroll.py::test_slow_scroll_goes_down`;
  and the click aimed before the header compacts in `test_send.py`.
