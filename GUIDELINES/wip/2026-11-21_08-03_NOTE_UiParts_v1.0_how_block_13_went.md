# NOTE UiParts v1.0: how block 13 went

Where Block 13, Personal, went differently from the plan,
the choices taken in the run, and what verified it.
It was built with the rest of AP UnsavedMarks' Block 5,
whose NOTE says how that block ended.

**Block 13 was built before Block 11,** as Block 12 was.
The log's block waits for AP LogCursor's change to `log.js`,
its pull request #388, still open;
so the log, `log.js` and the log's sign are still plain.

## Different from the plan

1. **Who did it.**
   A sub-agent on Opus 5.5 wrote it in the session's own worktree,
   without a commit,
   card by card, a recorded tree after each.
   Two independent readers on Opus read the recorded trees,
   one My profile, the shared tokens, identities and `me`,
   My settings, `login.js` and the Go guard,
   the other the Appearance card with the scheme editor,
   Notifications, the marks, the tests and the OpenAPI file,
   and then checked each rework.
   Two rounds of rework followed;
   the commits were made from recorded trees.
2. **Four steps, not one commit:**
   the plan names one,
   "fix(ui): Personal settings keep what is typed";
   the block is a commit per card,
   "fix(ui): My profile keeps what is typed",
   "fix(ui): My settings keep what is chosen",
   "fix(ui): The appearance and the scheme editor keep an edit"
   and "fix(ui): Notifications keep a rule being written",
   each with its CHANGELOG fragment.
3. **The own password's form moved from `login.js` into My profile's part:**
   its fields are My profile's;
   the plan names `login.js` in Block 14.
4. **`theme.js` was touched,**
   though the brief said it stays plain:
   the builder had first loosened `TestUIPage`
   to let a plain script look up an id a part draws.
   The first reading refused that;
   the guard is as it was,
   and `theme.js` lost its writes into the card alone.
5. **Beyond the plain page, in Notifications:**
   - a read that finds a rule's save waiting keeps the rules shown;
     the plain card took the server's older rules and then sent those;
   - the rules are off until this session's first read has answered:
     in the block's first tree a change before it
     sent the defaults over the stored rules,
     and the plain card sent no rules at all.
6. **Beyond the plain page, at a sign-out:**
   a scheme's import read across a sign-out is dropped,
   and a late answer for the previous user draws nothing.
   Before, the static fields kept their text,
   and the scheme editor stayed open for the next user.
7. **No "changed meanwhile" notice**
   on the poll and the notification values:
   the plain fields had none,
   and leaving the field for Take theirs would save what is typed.
8. **My profile's requests** are those of the plain scripts,
   in this order: the user,
   the enabled providers where none are known yet,
   then the sessions and keys and the identities together.

## What the second reading found

The first reading's 9 and 14 points,
and in the second round one must left, in the tests.
The must points, and what a user would have met:

1. **The guard loosened** for `theme.js` (4 above).
2. **The import check's pattern missed real dynamic imports**
   after an operator, such as `=import(`.
3. **In the app, My profile's linked accounts were named by the provider's key:**
   the list filtered for the Link buttons was also the names' source.
4. **The current password alone counted as a change:**
   a password manager filling it would have marked the form,
   and Sign out would have asked.
5. **A rule's save waiting at a sign-out** had no test,
   and was dropped where it could be sent (D205).
6. **"A read that finds a save waiting" had no test.**
7. **A held-answer test could pass before the late answer was handled,**
   the fault Block 12's readers found.
8. **A change before the first read sent the defaults**
   over the user's stored rules, in the block's first tree (5 above).
9. **In the second round:**
   the test of "a read that finds a change waiting"
   needed the held answer within the save's one second,
   and could pass emptily or fail falsely on a loaded runner.
   It and the two other tests of the rule's save
   now run under Playwright's paused clock.
   `TestPartsImportSpec`'s table got the two forms without a space.

Done of the shoulds:
`useHelp`, `BusyButton`'s `data`,
`Notice` and `ResultLine` in place of copies,
the three builders of `components.js` marked (D181-A),
tests of Save as and of the drafts dropped,
a message's and a fragment's words.

## Seen and left

- **A scheme deleted elsewhere while it is edited** counts as changed,
  and Reset goes to the default, as on the plain page.
- **A notification read that fails**
  leaves the rules off until a later read succeeds.
- **A click on Close right after a refused hex value can miss:**
  the refusal's line moves the button,
  as the plain editor did.
- **`FileButton`'s `id`, `inputId` and `inputLabel`** have one caller:
  they are the plain page's ids and label.
- **The previews' ids,** `icon-window-preview` and `icon-radio-preview`,
  have no user since `theme.js` stopped filling them;
  kept as the plain page's ids.
- **The two held-answer tests** wait for the response's body and two frames:
  the page shows nothing when it drops an answer,
  so there is no signal to wait on;
  only a clock-driven run would make them exact.
- **`logout` awaits its sends without a timeout,**
  the waiting settings since before and the waiting rule now:
  a server that accepts the connection and never answers
  holds the sign-out.
  A refusal, a 401 or a server gone do not.
- **The same user's sign-in after an expired session**
  sends the waiting rule once;
  its path is the plain page's, and it has no test of its own.
- **The guard's list** still holds `sign-in` alone;
  the Personal regions were never on it.

## Decisions

Taken in the run under the owner's `EXEC++`, each the owner's to change:

- **D203.**
  My profile reads when its page is shown and after its own changes,
  not at a stream event naming `user.update`, `token.*` or `identity.*`:
  those come for the page's own changes too,
  and following them would read twice.
  The other way: read at them while shown,
  for a change made in another browser.
- **D204.**
  The scheme editor's Save as is off while the editor holds no change,
  and Enter in the name follows it.
  The plain button was always on,
  so a built-in scheme could be kept as one's own unchanged.
  The other way: the plain button, always on.
- **D205.**
  A notification rule changed in the second before Sign out
  is sent first, under the user's own token,
  by one line of `logout` beside the waiting settings;
  it is dropped only where another user signs in after an expired session,
  and sent once for the same user.
  On main's page it was sent under whoever signed in next
  (`forgetSession`, the 401, `holdPushes` with no user, `resumePushes`).
  The other way: drop it at Sign out, as the first hand-back did.

Not a decision: the editor's Reset puts the name back too,
as the plain one did.

## For the blocks still to come

- **New or extended in `shared/`, and when to use it:**
  - `me`, `own`, `meChanged` (`me.ts`):
    who is in and the token in use, as a signal,
    for any part that draws by the user;
  - `userPath` and the requests of `account.ts`:
    a user's tokens and identities, by the caller's runner;
  - `Tokens` and `Identities`:
    a user's sessions and keys and its identities,
    with the caller's runner, counter and forms;
  - `useHelp`: a help's button and explanation as a pair,
    where a group or a form has one help;
  - `SaveButton`: a form's Save by its state, outside `FormFooter`;
  - `FormFooter`'s `after`: a control after the form's buttons;
  - `Select`'s `before` and `after`:
    a drawing between the label and the choice, or a line after it;
  - `Switch`'s `aria` and `data`, `BusyButton`'s `data`:
    a name for a screen reader, and the attributes the page or a test reads;
  - `NumberField`'s `onChange`: told as the field is left;
  - `FileButton`'s `id`, `inputId` and `inputLabel`:
    where the page names the button and its input.
- **Plain names left, and why:**
  - `renderLangSelect` of `prefs.js` fills the sign-in's language,
    and `applyAppearance` of `appearance.js` sets the sign-in's mode:
    both stay plain with the sign-in until Block 14;
  - `notify.js`' `takeNotifySave`, `saveNotifyRules` and `sendNotifySave`
    are what `login.js` calls,
    `holdPushes`, `resumePushes` and `logout`;
    `renderNotify` is what `app.js` calls at a change of language.
- **What Block 14's sign-in half inherits:**
  the new password's pair and the language's options
  have a second user in the sign-in;
  the sign-in's mode menu has the Appearance card's three options;
  `logout` sends a waiting rule with the waiting settings (D205).
- **`theme.js` no longer draws in the card:**
  a plain script must never look up an id a part draws;
  the guard refuses it, and a region is plain or a part, not both.
- **Test lessons:**
  - a test that depends on a timer of the page,
    as a rule's one-second save,
    runs under Playwright's paused clock,
    and every helper after the pause that waits on a timer or a frame
    needs the clock run or resumed first;
  - a held answer is waited for until the page has taken it,
    not only until it was fulfilled;
  - a changelog bullet is at most three lines and one clause:
    the fourth step's first bullet, of four lines and two clauses,
    was refused at the commit and became two fragments.
