# NOTE UiParts v1.0: how block 14 began

Where the first half of Block 14, Shell, went differently from the plan,
the choices taken in the run, and what verified it.
This half makes ☰, the pages and their addresses, and the page's chrome parts;
the second half makes the sign-in parts:
`login.js`' dialog, the first owner's form and the open banner.

**This half was built before Block 11,** as Blocks 12 and 13 were.
The log's block waited for AP LogCursor's change to `log.js`,
its pull request #388, which reached main while this half was in its rework;
so in this half `log.js`, the log's page and its address are still plain,
and Block 11 follows it.

## Different from the plan

1. **Two halves and two pull requests for one block:**
   this one, then the sign-in.
   The plan has one block and names one commit.
2. **A design turn before any code.**
   The builder read and handed back a design with seven questions,
   changing no project file; the coordinating session answered, then it built.
   The questions:
   who draws Sign out, Select server and the personal row
   (the chrome, from `me`, D206);
   ☰'s log entry while `log.js` is plain
   (drawn from what `log.js` hands it);
   how the regions ask about the page
   (they import the store, with a read that does not subscribe,
   and a table of every call site and its form);
   the cards (their frames skeleton, D207);
   `currentView` (a read-only getter);
   My profile's hidden card (the rule once, in the parts);
   the changelog of the steps a user does not see
   (one fragment, amended step by step).
3. **Who built it and who read it.**
   A sub-agent on Opus 5.5 wrote it in the session's own worktree,
   without a commit, a recorded tree per step.
   Two independent readers on Opus read the recorded trees,
   one the pages' store, `pages.js`, the call sites that ask about the page,
   ☰, who is in and Sign out with `login.js`, and the shared parts extended,
   the other the cards and badges, the rows, the header's scroll code,
   `components.js` with the connect page, the guard and the tests,
   the trees, the changelog and the documents;
   then both checked the rework.
   Two rounds of rework followed;
   the commits were made from recorded trees.
4. **Five steps, not one commit:**
   the plan names "refactor(ui): The menu, the pages and the sign-in are parts";
   this half is
   "refactor(ui): The section cards' badges and folds are the parts'",
   "refactor(ui): The header compacts and goes back to top from the parts",
   "refactor(ui): The pages and their addresses are a store",
   "refactor(ui): The main menu and the page buttons are parts"
   and "fix(ui): The settings pages' rows keep their sections menu open".
   The four refactor steps share one fragment under Changed,
   amended step by step;
   the fifth adds two under Fixed.
5. **`login.js` was touched by this half** (D206),
   though the builder's brief kept it for the sign-in's half:
   ☰ cannot be a part while a plain script writes into it.
6. **The cards' frames stay skeleton** (D207):
   the plan names the section cards among the page's chrome;
   only their badges, pills and fold's functions moved.
7. **Beyond the plain page:**
   - a settings page's sections menu stays open, with the focus where it was,
     when who is in is read again or the language changes;
     before, the row's plain builder emptied the menu and filled it again,
     `renderMe` at every `loadMe`, every row at a change of language;
   - a section opened from a settings page's menu, or from a pill,
     no longer slips under the page's row after a change of page,
     which main's page had for some pairs of pages;
   - the personal row's Sign out is hidden until `me` is known;
     the plain one showed while still signed out, behind the sign-in.

## Decisions

Taken in the run under the owner's `EXEC++`, each the owner's to change:

  Who is in, Sign out, Forget key and Select server
  are drawn by the chrome from the signal `me` in this half,
  so `login.js` loses that drawing before its own half:
  ☰ cannot be a part while a plain script writes into it,
  and the tests hold ☰'s Sign out equal to the row's.
  The other way: `login.js` keeps drawing the personal row
  and hands ☰ its label.
- **D207.**
  The section cards' frames stay skeleton markup;
  only the badges, the pills and the fold's functions are the parts'.
  The other way: a shared `Card` part each region draws,
  its body moved into TSX, which is a rewrite of every region.

Taken with the design, not numbered:
- the block in two halves, two pull requests, a design turn first;
- ☰ draws the log's entry from what `log.js` hands it, while `log.js` is plain;
- the regions import the pages' store, and `isShown` does not subscribe,
  so that a change of page adds no request;

- the changelog of the four steps that change nothing a user sees
  is one fragment under Changed, amended step by step.

## For the blocks still to come

- **How a region tells its card's head something:** one way,
  `cardBadges(cardId, [{text, kind}])` of `shared/card.ts`,
  a store write with no DOM, `[]` to clear;
  `showCard(cardId)` to bring a card into view.
  No element is looked up, and `shared/card.ts` knows no region:
  its caller hands it the card's id.
  A plain script hands its badges over as data,
  `MassimoParts.chrome.badges`, as `cubes.js` does.
- **How a part asks about the page** (`shared/pages.ts`):
  - `isShown(view)` asks at that moment and subscribes to nothing:
    for a handler, a reader, a timer, an event,
    so that a change of page sends no request the page did not send;
  - `page` is the signal, for a drawing that follows the page;
    only the chrome's drawings read it now;
  - `onView(view, fn)` runs once per showing,
    on the `viewshown` event, as before;
  - `showView`, `mayShow` and `setAddress` as they were.
  The store names no element:
  the chrome hands it the pages and the cards with addresses (`definePages`).
- **A plain script hands the store an address of its own**
  by `MassimoParts.pages.ownAddress(view, {read, write, shown, opened})`,
  as `pages.js`' `initPages` does for the log;
  Block 11 moves that into the log's part.
- **Plain names left, and why:**
  - `showView`, `isShown`, `mayShow`, `showAddress`, `setAddress`
    and the getter `currentView` in `pages.js`:
    the calls of other plain scripts and of the tests, each a call of the store;
  - `logHash` and `readLogHash`:
    they read the log's plain state, until Block 11;
  - `showCard(card, instant)` of `components.js`, for `home.js`;
  - `brandMarks` and the menus' plumbing
    (`dropdown`, `menuArrows`, `placeMenu`) and the builders:
    `connect.html` loads `components.js` and no `parts.js`;
    `shared/brand.tsx` is a second copy of the mark,
    and each names the other.
- **Who is in, as a signal:**
  `me` of `shared/me.ts`;
  `whoOf(me)` words it for the row, ☰ and the dialog before a sign-out;
  `profileOf(me)` is My profile's rule,
  with `me.peek()` where a part must not follow it.
- **At a sign-out the plain `state.me` and the signal `me` differ**
  between `forgetSession`'s first line and `signedOut()`,
  while the signed-out language loads.
  A part that sends a request by `me` in that window
  sends what the plain page did not;
  the sign-in half decides where `me` is cleared.
- **Shared parts extended,** and when to use them:
  `MenuItem`'s `id`, `labelId`, `data`, `title`, `aria`, `after`, `current`,
  where the page or a test names an entry;
  `Icon`'s `useId`, an icon with an id;
  `Copy`'s `id`.
- **Test lessons:**
  - a value all of several parts write, as the sticky row's height,
    is written by the one shown alone,
    never by a hidden one;
  - a guard that counts reads counts every request under `/api/v1/` per showing,
    against a table recorded on the page before,
    with a line saying how it was taken,
    so that a read added by any region fails it;
  - the intermediate trees are run in a browser too:
    a commit of the plan is a working page;
  - a rest position is tested with a card that can reach it,
    one with page enough below it,
    and the check shown failing by itself.
- **AP MobileApp's pull request #390** meets this half and the next.
  It is open and in conflict with main since Block 13 removed `profile.js`.
