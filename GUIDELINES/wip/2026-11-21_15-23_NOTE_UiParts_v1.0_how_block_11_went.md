# NOTE UiParts v1.0: how block 11 went

Where Block 11, Log, went differently from the plan,
the choices taken in the run, and what verified it.
The block makes the log's page a part:
its head with the search, the six choices, Clear and Mark all as seen,
its lines, a command's detail and Older,
its store and requests, every wording of a line,
the problems not yet seen and the log's address.

**This block was built after Blocks 12, 13 and the first half of 14.**
It waited for AP LogCursor's change to `log.js`,
its pull request #388
("feat(ui/log): The log of every Cube polls forward from its newest line"),
which reached main on 2026-11-21.

## What it made

- **`ui/src/log/`,** the region:
  - `index.tsx`: `MassimoParts.log`, the mount,
    the log's address handed to the pages' store (`ownAddress("log", …)`),
    `signedOut` and `serverEvent`;
  - `state.ts`: `logState`;
    `lines.ts`: a line and its local type, `LogEvent`;
    `text.ts`: every wording of a line, line for line from `log.js`;
    `kinds.ts`: `LOG_KINDS`;
  - `store.ts`: the reads, the poll as a read on from the newest line,
    the live lines in one batch, the view followed in place,
    the line read first kept in its place,
    the session's number after every await, the focus after Mark seen;
  - `choices.ts`; `alerts.ts`, the problems not yet seen,
    with ☰'s sign handed over by `MassimoParts.chrome.log` as before;
    `address.ts`, `logHash` and `readLogHash` from `pages.js`;
  - `blocks.ts`, `Log.tsx`, `Line.tsx`,
    Mark seen a part of its own, drawn for a problem's line alone.
- **The lines are drawn in blocks of 200 by their keys,**
  the blocks cut over the lines' own order, a kept element per line.
  A line's key is its Cube's serial with the event's id;
  a line that came by the stream has its sequence,
  and keeps that key when a later load brings the same event:
  the same time, kind and device.
- **`log.js`** went from 1544 lines to 114:
  the plain names `app.js`, `tiles.js` and the UI tests call,
  each a call of `MassimoParts.log`,
  and the page globals `logState`, `logAlerts` and `LOG_KINDS`,
  getters onto the part's state.
  72 names left it, none with a caller left.
  `partials/log.html`, the template, is gone;
  `partials/log-view.html` is the section alone.
- **`pages.js`** lost `logHash`, `readLogHash` and `setAddress`;
  `initPages` wires back and forward alone.
- **Shared:**
  `shared/field.tsx`'s `Select` gains `aria` and `selectCls`,
  its `TextField` `row` and `aria`;
  `model/device.ts` gains `rfLabel`, which the tiles call too.
- **`TestLogKinds`** reads `ui/src/log/kinds.ts`
  and also fails where a kind has no text.
- **The line's type is local:**
  the API file has no schema of a log line, and `docs/ui.md` says so.
- **Unchanged:** `app.js`, `tiles.js` and `login.js`.
- **Tests:**
  `test_log_updates.py`, new, 33 tests per case on the last tree;
  the log's test lines and a stub of the server's log, `StubLog`,
  in `helpers.py`;
  the guard's region `log` keeps its head's views,
  and a second region, `log-lines`,
  holds a problem's Mark seen in focus and a command's detail open.
  `test_log.py` and `test_log_alerts.py` are unchanged, not a line:
  a part draws in the microtask after a call,
  before the next command of the test.

## Different from the plan

1. **The order:** Block 11 came after Blocks 12, 13 and the first half of 14,
   having waited for AP LogCursor's #388.
2. **A design turn before any code,** as Block 14's halves had.
   The builder handed back a design with seven questions,
   changing no project file;
   the coordinating session answered, then it built.
   The answers:
   the whole region moves, not the drawing alone;
   ☰'s sign stays a call of `MassimoParts.chrome.log`;
   a Cube's log follows the stream's `resync` (D208);
   `logState` and `logAlerts` stay page globals onto the store;
   a line's key is its event's id with its Cube's serial,
   and a line once shown keeps its key while it is the same event;
   a sign-out forgets all but the choices (D209);
   `setAddress` goes from `pages.js`.
   The answer added eight points:
   a scratch check of the texts beyond the tests;
   one home in `src/model/` for what is no region's;
   the local line type said in `docs/ui.md`;
   two more measurements,
   and a stop to hand back where they missed the builder's own limits;
   the sign-out's forgetting tested with held answers;
   a test of a timer under Playwright's paused clock;
   the whole suite in two cases on the last tree,
   `test_shell_updates.py`'s table of reads unchanged;
   `app.js`, `tiles.js` and `login.js` unchanged.
3. **Who built it and who read it.**
   A sub-agent on Opus 5.5 built it in the session's own worktree,
   without a commit, a recorded tree per step.
   Two independent readers on Opus read the recorded trees through git
   while the builder ran its last tests:
   one the store and its requests, the keys, the scroll's steps,
   the address, the unseen problems' state, the sign-out, the stream
   and what is left of `log.js`;
   the other the drawing, the blocks, the texts,
   the shared parts and `model/`, the guard, the tests and the documents;
   then both checked the rework, in two rounds.
   The commits were made from recorded trees.
4. **Three steps, not one commit:**
   the plan names "fix(ui): The log keeps the focus and a command's fold";
   this block is that,
   "fix(ui): The log forgets its lines at a sign-out"
   and "fix(ui): A Cube's log reads on when the stream starts over",
   with four fragments under Fixed.
5. **Beyond the plain page:**
   - a view scrolled down keeps the line read first where it stood
     when every line is drawn again,
     which put it back at the top before;
     where lines come in and go at the bottom it stays as the plain page kept it,
     now by an anchor;
   - after Mark seen the focus goes to the next problem's Mark seen,
     below, else above, else the search;
     before, it fell to the page;
   - a sign-out, and another user after an expired session,
     forget the log of the user who was in (D209);
     before, the plain page forgot nothing of it;
   - at the stream's `resync` a Cube's own log reads on from its newest line,
     keeping the place and the older pages (D208);
     before, the lines the stream missed showed only after a reload;
   - the who choice follows another user who comes in without a sign-out,
     after the first owner is made on an open server or a key is pasted;
     main's page kept the first user's list until a reload.
6. **The measurements were accepted as they are.**
   The builder stopped once before its last runs,
   as it had been told to, to hand in its measurements.
   One line added with 5000 shown misses the builder's own limit of 8 ms,
   even with the blocks the design named as its fallback,
   and is half of what the plain page takes;
   there is no virtualising.

## What the second reading found

The first reading:
both readers confirmed the move where they compared line for line;
two musts were found.

1. **A view scrolled down jumped by a line whenever a line was cut at the bottom.**
   The height was read before the cut lines were gone,
   where the plain code removed them first:
   at every new line in the default range, the last 20, and at the cap of 2000,
   what a user read moved down a line.
   Now the first line in the box's view is put back where it stood.
2. **The resync's load, D208 as first built,**
   cut off a load or an Older of the page's own,
   and threw away the older pages and the user's place.
   It reads forward now, as the poll does under All Cubes,
   and not while the page's own flow is under way, but once after it.

Done of the first round's shoulds:
the blocks cut over the stable order of the lines,
so that a line that stays shown is never built anew at a filter's change;
held answers waited for with the response finished and the page's own promise,
no timed loop;
the keys handed out with the kept lines' keys known,
one rule for the same event;
the problems' levels dropped with their lines;
`upperFirst` in the log's texts;
`TextField` named by `aria` as its siblings;
the test kit in `helpers.py`;
stale comments;
the first fragment's words;
a batch of lines built into the view once;
Mark seen's focus.

The second round:
both readers verified the rework.
One reader found no must left;
the other one, in what the rework had added:

3. **A resync with no stored line held**
   asked for the newest page itself and put it above the live lines,
   out of order, the error's line staying under it.
   That is the "cannot load" line after a load made while the server was down,
   or a log that loaded empty with live lines since:
   a user who opened the log while the server restarted
   would have seen older lines above newer ones.
   There the log is loaded again now.

Done with it:
the focus's mark cleared whenever it was wanted and at a sign-out,
and read by a problem's line alone;
the anchor never a line that is cut;
the guard taking its routes away in a `finally`
and reading the problems again;
the users' list read again where another user is in than the one it was read for,
after the first owner is created on an open server or a key pasted,
which main's page had too,
with a fragment of its own.

## Seen and left

- **Older clicked while a read on or a poll runs is not held back,**
  so its put-back of the scroll can be off by the lines the read added;
  under All Cubes the poll and Older have overlapped so since before this block.
- **The test that a line that stays shown is never built anew at a filter's change**
  is a forward guard:
  its stub ignores the kind, so it does not reach the old fault;
  the new cut cannot split a block by its construction.
- **`tiles.js` repeats the fallback for a room's name** (`log.room`).
- **The who choice's paths without a sign-out before**
  (the first owner made on an open server,
  a key into a dialog no start waited for)
  cannot be made in the suite, whose server has users:
  the fix covers them by reading who is in, and no test reaches them.

## Decisions

Taken in the run under the owner's `EXEC++`, each the owner's to change:

- **D208.**
  A Cube's log shown reads on at the stream's `resync`,
  a gap AP LogCursor's run had found and left.
  As first built it loaded the log again;
  the first reading showed that this cut off a load or an Older of the page's own
  and lost the user's place,
  so it reads forward as the poll does under All Cubes,
  not while the page's own flow is under way;
  where it holds no stored line it loads, there being no place to read on from;
  where the server's log ends before the newest line, or past ten pages,
  it loads again.
  The other way: left as the plain page has it.
- **D209.**
  A sign-out forgets everything of the log but the choices,
  which are the address:
  the lines and the paging, the search, the users,
  the commands' details and which are open, every Cube's rooms,
  the lines' blocks, the unseen problems and whose marks they were;
  an answer still on its way lands nowhere.
  The other way: the choices back to their defaults too,
  the who choice naming a user.

Taken with the design, not numbered:
- the whole region moved, not the drawing alone;
- `logState` and `logAlerts` stay page globals onto the store,
  so the tests are unchanged, until Block 15 gives the tests one handle;
- ☰ is handed its sign by `MassimoParts.chrome.log` as before;
- a line once shown keeps its key while it is the same event;
- `setAddress` gone from `pages.js`.

## For the blocks still to come

What the sign-in half of Block 14 and Block 15 must know:

- **The log's plain names left in `log.js`,** and why:
  they are what `app.js`, `tiles.js` and the UI tests call,
  each a call of `MassimoParts.log`;
  `logState`, `logAlerts` and `LOG_KINDS` are getters onto the part's state,
  so that the tests read them as before.
  Block 15 gives the tests one handle, and then they go.
- **`rfLabel` is `model/device.ts`',**
  and `tiles.js` reaches it through the log's handle
  until Block 15 gives it a home of its own.
- **A region hands the pages' store its own address:**
  `pages.js` holds none now;
  the log's region calls `ownAddress("log", {read, write, shown, opened})`
  of `shared/pages.ts` as its module loads,
  before the page's first `showAddress`.
- **An event's read waits for the page's own flow:**
  a stream's event that comes while the page's own load or Older is under way
  is noted, and run once afterwards,
  in the same generation and session, the page still shown.
  It reads on where there is something to read on from,
  and loads where there is not.
- **A list of many items** is drawn in blocks cut over the items' own order,
  with a kept element per item,
  so that a filter or a load never moves an item that stays into another block.
- **The scroll is kept by an anchor:**
  the line read first is put back where it stood after the drawing,
  taken among the lines that stay.
- **A focus handed to the next item** is given in a layout effect,
  by a mark only that kind of item reads,
  cleared whenever it was wanted, the item there or not, and at a sign-out.
- **Test lessons:**
  - a stub of the server's log and its lines are in `helpers.py`
    (`StubLog`, `log_lines`, `isolate_log`),
    for every test file and the guard;
  - a held answer is waited for with the response finished
    and the page's own promise, never a timed loop;
  - a guard region's routes are taken away in a `finally`;
  - where texts move, every wording is compared against the plain page
    over a corpus, in every language, with a run shown failing;
  - a measurement is taken against the plain page under the same load.
