# NOTE UiParts v1.0: how block 14 ended

Where the second half of Block 14, the sign-in, went differently from the plan,
the choices taken in the run, and what verified it.
This half makes the page's door parts:
`login.js`' dialog, the first owner's form and the open server's warning.
It ends the block whose first half, the page's shell,
is in the NOTE "how block 14 began".

**This half was built after AP MobileApp's pull request #390,**
whose Block 14 touches the same card, in the order the owner set:
the first half, then #390, then this half.

## What the readings found

The first reading: both readers confirmed
that the session's logic, OPAQUE and the pushes changed only where the design named,
that the card matches the plain markup element for element,
and that no password is kept in a store.
Four musts and thirteen shoulds; the musts:

1. **The late answers left second requests unguarded.**
   A device's name after its settings, a copy's programme after its configuration,
   the virtual thermostat's detail after its write;
   and the worst: the House panel in Eco or Comfort,
   whose read of a preset is sent again after a sign-in
   and was followed by the setpoint's write.
   After an expired session and another user's sign-in
   that write went out under the new user's key,
   a write the new user never made.
   Each second request checks the session now;
   on its second walk the builder found one more, `shared/put.tsx` after its reload.
2. **The late answers had tests for two of ten rows,** for a sign-out alone:
   now ten scenarios, each in both ways
   (a sign-out; an expired session and another user's sign-in),
   each shown failing on the tree before the step for its own reason.
3. **A test showed that nothing more is sent by sleeping:**
   it counts the posts once the first creation has ended now.
4. **The first half's parts drew their first texts in English**
   on a page in another language until something drew them again:
   fixed before this half, as #395.
   Its first try did nothing on an English page,
   a signal set to the value it had telling nobody;
   hence the signal's empty start.

Done of the shoulds:
the tiles shown before the wait for the language;
two checks that could not fail dropped, a test's words fitted to the new order;
the card's test changing the language for real;
the card's ☰ and `ModeSelect` reading the language;
one version row for both ☰;
the late answers' tests waiting until the page has taken the answer;
a guard on Unlisted's line that did nothing taken out;
exports nobody used;
the first owner's fields with the plain form's attributes;
the form's going at a failed read of who is in said;
the README's list of tests.

The second reading:
one reader found everything settled;
the other one must, a guarded write without a test,
a device's name after its settings, whose docstring said it needed none,
and four small shoulds, in tests and one sentence of `docs/ui.md`.
Fixed; the coordinating session read that change itself
and rewrapped one docstring in the recorded trees.

## Decisions

Taken in the run, each the owner's to change:

- **D210.** The card's fields are not controlled:
  no draw sets what they hold, so a password manager's fill stays;
  they are read at the submit and emptied at each opening alone, never at the closing,
  since the app's autofill takes the values when the form goes.
- **D211.** A sign-out forgets who is in for the plain code and the parts in one turn,
  and everything else, before it waits for the signed-out language;
  `setToken(null)` stays where it was.
- **D212.** D197 closed for the regions moved before Block 10:
  each takes the session's number before its request
  and drops what follows where a sign-out raised it,
  a second request of its own included.
- **D213.** Create the owner stays busy while the app keeps the login:
  in the app a second tap created the owner again and said the name was taken.
- **D214.** The forty `hasCap` sites of the regions moved before are not moved here,
  but in Block 15, with the page's data as signals.
- **D224,** the owner's, through AP MobileApp's D60-A:
  on a touch screen no field takes the focus when the card opens or the way is switched;
  both ways' forms share one place of one height.

Taken with the design, not numbered:
- the dialog element stays the skeleton's, the part draws into it;
- AP SignInResend's behaviour after another user's sign-in unchanged;
- one check of a new password typed twice, the mismatch first:
  the first owner's form, with the first password empty and the second typed,
  now says that the two differ.
