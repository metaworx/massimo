# NOTE UiParts v1.0: how block 15 went

Where Block 15, End, went differently from the plan,
the choices taken in the run, and what verified it.
It is the plan's last block:
the page's data are signals, the counters and the forwarding scripts are gone,
the builders nobody calls are gone,
and the project contract's bullet "The web UI" says what the page now is.

## What it made

Sixteen commits on the branch, where the plan names two:

1. "test(ui-test): The reads each data source causes, per page":
   `test_data_updates.py`, recorded before any change and unchanged after it,
   but for the rows a decision changes.
2. "refactor(ui): The settings are signals".
3. "refactor(ui): The parts read who is in from one signal":
   53 calls in 27 part files; five rules in `shared/me.ts`, line for line with `login.js`.
4. "refactor(ui): The Cubes' list is a signal".
5. "fix(security:ui): A plain load answered after a sign-out changes nothing",
   from the second reading (below).
6. "refactor(ui): The rooms are signals", each room a live item.
7. "refactor(ui): The regions' stores draw without counters":
   38 counter bumps, every region's counter and `shared/counter.ts` gone.
8. "refactor(ui): The plain names that only called a part are gone":
   ten forwarding scripts gone, so the page loads 15 scripts where it loaded 25;
   the UI tests reach the page through one handle, `window.MassimoParts`,
   their calls renamed by a script that can run again.
9. "refactor(ui): The plain builders nobody calls are gone":
   22 builders of `components.js`; the room's and the device's facts to `src/model/`;
   five one-element partials inlined; `tools/ui-split/` gone;
   the ages follow a minute signal, where a plain script wrote into the parts.
10. "fix(ui): The focus is given in the drawing that shows its place":
    eight focuses moved from an effect to a layout effect or the handler;
    the leaving dialog settles in a layout effect too.
11. "docs(contract): The web UI after the move to parts":
    the contract's bullet rewritten, v1.17.0, with its two generated copies;
    `docs/ui.md` "How the page is built" written anew; `docs/ui-testing.md` v1.22.0.
12. "test(ui-test): The late Older test counts the first user's lines alone",
    a test of Block 11 that failed once in a while under the whole suite (below).
13. "merge: main into the web UI's last block, before its pull request" (below).
14. "test(ui-test): The slow scroll test reads the content's movement" (below).
15. "docs(ui): How the page is built names Keep it open and its late answers"
    and
16. "test(ui-test): The guard holds Users on a server without users",
    both from the reading of the merge (below).

New guards: `TestStateWrittenWhole` and `TestWrittenInPlace`,
a field of the data written whole, never in place;
`TestUIPage` refusing a `document.querySelector` in a plain script, two exceptions;
`TestUINoHTMLInjection` refusing a `data-i18n` written by a script;
`test_sign_out_late.py`'s scenarios for the plain loads,
each asserting that nothing of the next user's page is busy, off or turning.

## Different from the plan

1. **Twelve steps and two tests' corrections, not two commits:**
   the plan names "refactor(ui): The page's data are signals"
   and "refactor(ui): The plain builders nobody calls are gone".
2. **A design turn first,** a design note of ten steps with its questions,
   changing no project file; the coordinating session answered, D215 to D223.
   The build waited for the sign-in half of Block 14 to reach main;
   then a short delta turn read the design again against the merged tree
   and asked two more questions, D233 and D234.
3. **Built in two parts** by a sub-agent on Opus 5.5 in the session's worktree,
   a recorded tree per step:
   the data's steps and who is in first,
   handed back for two readers while the builder went on, then the rest.
4. **The order of the data's steps** changed in the build:
   the settings and who is in before the Cubes and the rooms,
   since a counter can only go once everything it stood for is a signal.
5. **Beyond the plan:**
   the security fix of the plain loads, a step of its own from the second reading;
   two tests that failed once in a while, each the test's fault, corrected
   (step 12 and the last commit).
6. **The merge of another plan:**
   AP FirstOwner, four blocks on the sign-in card and its neighbours,
   was built beside this block by this session and reached main first;
   this block merged main once, at the end.

## Decisions

Taken in the run, each the owner's to change:

- **D215.** A room is a live item;
  the readers across all rooms read the devices untracked and follow the array.
  The other way: the per-room counter kept, against the plan's text.
- **D216.** The pairing takes the shown Cube's rooms from the page's: one read fewer.
  The other way: its own read, as before.
- **D217.** A script that only forwards goes.
  The other way: a forwarding name stays where a plain script calls it.
- **D218.** Facts and texts only parts call move to `src/model/`;
  the rooms' actions stay plain.
  The other ways: all stay; all into `src/rooms/`.
- **D219.** The signals of the data and the update's state live in `src/shared/`,
  the coordinating session's correction of the builder's `src/model/`,
  which holds no state.
  The other way: the update's state stays plain.
- **D220.** `window.MassimoParts` is the tests' one handle,
  an entry only the suite calls marked so.
  The other way: a separate handle for the tests.
- **D221.** Who is in read again and the settings applied
  are sources for every region of the guard.
  The other way: only where a region reads them.
- **D222.** The partials that were only a slot are in `index.html`.
  The other way: they stay partials.
- **D223.** The parts' lookups of the skeleton's message lines are left and named.
  The other way: the parts draw their message lines.
- **D233.** The plain removal of a device without a room
  moves into the rooms' part with the session's check, and `unlisted.js` goes.
  The other ways: it stays plain and checks the token before its reload; left as named.
- **D234.** A Go test against writes in place, by regular expressions;
  what it cannot see is said in its comment.
  The other ways: frozen arrays in the setters; review alone.

The builder's departures from its design, each named in its step's message:
the order of the data's steps;
`state.version` stays plain;
the function is `isMe`, not `self`;
`canSync` stays `login.js`';
a live map's forgetting and clearing tell the parts through a signal.

From the readings: a late answer of a plain load settles and changes nothing,
neither hanging nor rejecting;
"a plain script draws nothing a part draws" made true for the ages and a tile's focus,
and what the plain code still finds in the whole page named:
the menus, the help slots, the static texts and the brand's marks.

The next free number is UiParts D235.

## What the readings found

Two independent readers on Opus, one the data,
the other the settings, who is in, the trees, the tests, the messages and the documents,
read the recorded trees through git four times and ran tests of their own in exports.
Three reworks followed, each fix put into the step that introduced what it fixes,
the later trees recorded anew.

**The first reading,** of the first five steps: four musts, fourteen shoulds.

1. **The House tile no longer followed the rooms' list:**
   it read the list untracked,
   so after a reload of the rooms without one of the Cubes its list stayed
   until the next poll.
   A reader's test failed 4 times on the step and passed two steps before.
2. **Put into a room could go to the wrong Cube:**
   after a change of Cube the form had the new serial
   over the old Cube's device and rooms until the rooms' read answered.
3. **`test_icon_choice.py` had stopped testing its fallback:**
   a setting can no longer be deleted the old way.
4. **The new user's role did not follow who is in,**
   older than the block, though the step claimed it followed.

**The second reading,** of the rest and the rework: four musts, seven shoulds.

1. **The contract's new bullet was not true of the tree:**
   two scripts missing from its list;
   "a plain script draws nothing a part draws" false in two places;
   the test it cited seeing less than claimed;
   "one place writes each list" not so;
   two rules of the old bullet lost.
   The coordinating session had read the bullet for sense and called it sound;
   the reader read it against the tree.
2. **`docs/ui.md` "How the page is built",** written anew,
   named about twenty plain functions that the block had removed.
3. **A load answered after a sign-out wrote the rooms of the user before:**
   the plain loads took no session.
   It became a step of its own, the security fix.
4. **Three more focuses were still given in an effect.**

**The third reading:** two musts, seven shoulds.

1. **The new step's late answer never settled:**
   both readers found, each with a test,
   that the next user's search for Cubes stayed on "Searching…",
   Refresh now kept turning, and the House panel's Send stayed busy.
   Now a late answer writes, draws and says nothing, and still settles,
   so that every awaiter's `finally` runs;
   the builder listed every awaiter of a plain load,
   and the plain chains that would go on from a load test its value.
2. **Two sentences of the bullet were still not so:**
   a focus given from a timer inside a click's handler,
   and the plain code finding the brand's marks and the texts in the whole page.

With it, a test of Block 11 that failed once in each of three whole-suite runs,
this block's and AP FirstOwner's, was shown to be the test's fault:
it counted every line of the log, also the next user's own live ones.
A reader showed the page right, a late answer waited for six seconds and none landing,
and the corrected test still failing on a planted line of the first user.

**The fourth reading:** settled, nothing open.
