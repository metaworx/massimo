## Block 4: UnconfirmedMarks

**The cause was not the one the Analysis guessed.**
The test did not read the devices tab before it was drawn:
it put the confirmation into the page's state alone (`updateDevice`),
and the server's own data takes that back.
A `state` or `config` event of the device brings the device as the server has it,
and every read of the rooms replaces it;
the server sends such events by itself after an earlier test's write,
at its next poll of the Cube, and in the reconnect it makes two minutes after a
write to read its Cube's budget.
The failure's screenshot shows the server's setpoint, 19.0°,
where the test had set 20.

- **The fix** tells the confirmation through the rooms read (the fixture
  `reads`), which the page takes by a read of its own,
  reads the rooms again after each event of the thermostat,
  and waits for the drawn setpoint and the notice's text before it compares.
  No product code changed: the server's data is right to win.
- **Its commit's summary** says so, not the plan's
  "waits for the devices tab":
  `test(ui-test): The unconfirmed marks test tells its confirmation through the rooms read`.
- **Proof:** a server event planted before the configuration's read
  (the reconnect's burst, or the thermostat's own `state` event)
  failed the old test 10 of 10 in 390-en, as CI did,
  and passed the new one 10 of 10 with each;
  `test_confirm.py` passed in the four cases, 12 of 12.
- **Left for later:** `docs/ui-testing.md`, "What the reads do not keep yet",
  names only a test's own `updateDevice` data.
  A device's own `state` and `config` events override what a test told
  through `reads` too; this test reads again after them, locally.
  A shared helper would cover the other tests, outside this plan.
