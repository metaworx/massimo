## Block 9: TimesAgo

- **Cause:** `SET_READ` wrote the Cube's read in place,
  and the list was drawn again only by the header's clock moving at the render.
  When the clock's tick came in the same millisecond,
  nothing moved and nothing was drawn.
  The list kept the tick's text: "4 sec. ago" at the first entry,
  and the earlier "5 sec. ago" at the second.
- **Ruled out:** the list's toggle coming late, a Cubes read answered
  meanwhile, `serverSkew` moving, flooring
  (32 diagnostic runs, loaded cores among them).
- **On the page's own clock:** 18 of 19 not drawn in the clock's millisecond;
  0 of 40 when written whole.
- **Fix,** in `test_header.py`: `SET_READ` writes the Cube's entry whole into
  `state.cubes`, as the page's loads do. The page is right.
- **Proof:** `Date.now()` held at one millisecond (PLANT=tick), 390-en.
  Old: 3 of 3 failed, "'2 sec. ago'". New: 3 of 3 passed.
  The file in the four cases: 80 passed.
