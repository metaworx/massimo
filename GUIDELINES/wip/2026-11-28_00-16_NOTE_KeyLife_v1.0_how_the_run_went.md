# NOTE KeyLife v1.0: how the run went

The owner's `PLAN++` of 2026-11-27 on the TL;DR gate (D1-A to D4-A,
D5-A narrowed) built all five blocks, five stacked pull requests;
D6-A to D8-A, added by the drafter, were taken as recommended.
Block 1 was merged as #477.
#481 (Block 2, on `main`), #482, #484 and #490 (Blocks 3 to 5) are open,
each on the one before:
#481 to #484 green, #481 and #482 after re-running an Android emulator job
that failed for reasons outside the code;
#490's checks were running when this NOTE was committed.

## How it was built

- An Opus sub-agent drafted the plan, others built the blocks in worktrees;
  the coordinating session reviewed, committed and opened the pull requests.
- Blocks 1 and 2 were built in parallel, Block 2 from `main`,
  then applied onto Block 1's branch, `docs/ui.md` keeping both rows' texts.
- Blocks 2 to 5 each had a second reader,
  whose confirmed faults a commit of their own fixed,
  each with a test seen failing first.
- Blocks 3 to 5 were committed on the very trees their builders tested.
- Locally ran the touched packages, `-race` of store and server,
  and the touched UI files in the four cases;
  the whole Go and UI suites were left to CI,
  by the owner's rule and the host's load (near 45 on 16 cores),
  not run as the plan's "whole UI suite once in one case".

## Per block

### The copy (Block 1, #477)

- The `Copy` is keyed by the key,
  so that one key's "Copied" never shows over the next,
  and stays in the hidden box with empty text,
  as `test_admin_updates.py` reads it.
- New kit helpers `grant_clipboard`, `copies` and `icon_after_last_line`
  (`docs/ui-testing.md` v1.30.0).
- On CI's runners the key fit one line at 1100 px and its icon wrapped below,
  which the helper refused; a `test(ui)` commit widened it before the merge.

### The gap (Block 2, #481)

- `Tokens` lists sessions under the rule, keys while not revoked,
  so an ended session is no longer listed.
- `massimo user revoke` failed with "no such token" on a key with a session;
  it now passes over a token its earlier revoke took.
- The change line's subject names the heirs,
  the log page drawing no field for them;
  `DeleteUser` logs them, its answer does not.
- Two migration tests follow the block of 100.

The second reader's three faults:

1. the settings file's key could be an heir, revoked with its maker at a reload,
   after which every start failed on the unique hash;
2. a key could be made under a maker revoked between admission and insert;
3. the bootstrap loop stopped at an heir labelled `MASSIMO_API_TOKEN`.

### The idle limit (Block 3, #482)

- `PUT {"idle_exempt": false}` on an integration's key is accepted,
  being its state;
  holding a key to the limit again needs who manages the user.
- New in the kit: `storekit.OpenAt` and `additiveMigration`.
- The second reader found only that `GET /api/v1/me` counted `idle_ends_at`
  from the previous use; fixed, with no bullet of its own (COMMIT.md §5.2).

### The end date (Block 4, #484)

- A passed date is 400 at `PUT` as at `POST`; the plan named `POST` alone.
- Change… offers the end date alone on an integration's key
  and on the own key of a user who manages nobody,
  changing two of Block 3's UI tests.
- "ends …" stands beside "ends … unless used" only where it comes first;
  `changeSummaries` joined the kit.

The second reader's three faults:

1. the settings file's key could end: the bootstrap kept its `expires_at`,
   the rule exempted it from the idle part alone,
   and its 409 lapsed once it ended;
2. year 0001 read as no end; now 400, a user's end too,
   with a Fixed bullet, since that predates KeyLife;
3. a half-typed date made a key without an end at Create key;
   the page now refuses it.

### The new secret (Block 5, #490)

Built on Block 4's first commit,
then moved onto its final head with `main` merged and tested there.
Beyond the plan:

- what the key itself subscribed to push goes with the old secret;
- the store refuses an integration's key too (`ErrIntegrationUser`, 403);
- no New secret on the key the page's session was made from,
  nor on an ended key;
- one `NewKey` schema for `POST` and the re-key, and three more texts.

After the error codes' commit, a third fixed four of the reader's findings:

1. an event stream opened with the old secret outlived it;
2. a session or an heir let in with the old secret just before the new one

## Open for the owner

- **D9.
  What a key's own holder may do to its life.**
  - **A (recommended).**
    A session made from a key counts as the key for its life:
    its end date, exclusion and new secret are 403 from it, as from the key;
    and `POST …/tokens` with an API key as bearer takes no `idle_exempt`.
    A leaked key can then neither sign in and lift its own end
    nor mint a key that never idles out.
  - **B.**
    As built (D4-A: a session is not the key).
- **D10.
  What the key's user may undo of a manager's choice.**
  - **A (recommended).**
    Lifting or removing an end date,
    and a new secret for a key kept from the idle limit,
    need who manages the user, as the exclusion does (D7-A);
    the key's user may still set or shorten an end.
  - **B.**
    As built.
- **D11.
  A new secret and the key's heirs.**
  - **A (recommended).**
    Heirs stay, as built: Revoke ends a key and its heirs,
    and New secret's question says how many it made.
  - **B.**
    A new secret also revokes the heirs.
- **D12.
  A new secret and the idle clock.**
  - **A (recommended).**
    A new secret counts as a use: it is handed to whoever will use the key.
  - **B.**
    As built: the last use stays.
- **D13.
  The app after a browser's new secret for the phone's key.**
  The phone's Turn off reports "revoked",
  while the key lives on with its new secret;
  with a password session the phone revokes it by id at once.
  - **A (recommended).**
    Leave the code: AP MobileApp's session takes it,
    and `docs/app.md` is to say so.
  - **B.**
    KeyLife changes the app.
